    How to cite¶

    If you use the package, please cite the paper that fits your work.

    The package itself. Smirnov, E. A. (2023). A new python package for identifying celestial bodies trapped in mean-motion resonances. Astronomy and Computing, 100707. doi:10.1016/j.ascom.2023.100707

    @article{Smirnov2023,
  title   = {A new python package for identifying celestial bodies trapped in mean-motion resonances},
  author  = {Smirnov, E. A.},
  journal = {Astronomy and Computing},
  year    = {2023},
  pages   = {100707},
  doi     = {10.1016/j.ascom.2023.100707}
}

    Secular resonances. Smirnov, E. A. (2026). Implementation of secular resonance support in the open-source python package "resonances". Astronomy and Computing, 54, 101022.

    @article{Smirnov2026,
  title   = {Implementation of Secular Resonance Support in the Open-Source Python Package ``Resonances''},
  author  = {Smirnov, Evgeny A},
  year    = {2026},
  journal = {Astronomy and Computing},
  volume  = {54},
  pages   = {101022}
}

    The libration analysis of 1.0 (statuses, causes, confidence). Smirnov, E. (2026). Physical Identification of Mean-Motion Resonances in Numerical Integrations: When a Librating Angle Is Not Enough. Submitted. The journal reference is not known yet: check this page or the repository before citing.

    @unpublished{Smirnov2026mmr,
  title  = {Physical Identification of Mean-Motion Resonances in Numerical Integrations: When a Librating Angle Is Not Enough},
  author = {Smirnov, Evgeny},
  year   = {2026},
  note   = {Submitted}
}

    Mass identification of mean-motion resonances.

    • Smirnov, E. A., & Dovgalev, I. S. (2018). Identification of asteroids in two-body resonances. Solar System Research, 52(4), 347–354. doi:10.1134/S0038094618040056
    • Smirnov, E. A., Dovgalev, I. S., & Popova, E. A. (2017). Asteroids in three-body mean motion resonances with planets. Icarus. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2017.09.032
    • Smirnov, E. A., & Shevchenko, I. I. (2013). Massive identification of asteroids in three-body resonances. Icarus, 222, 220–228.

    Applications.

    • Smirnov, E. (2025). A highly resonant Neptunian region: a systematic search for two-body and three-body mean-motion resonances. Icarus, 436, 116584. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2025.116584
    • Smirnov, E. (2025). Chaotic diffusion and transient resonance captures of the near-Earth asteroid 2024 YR4. Astronomy & Astrophysics, 699. doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202554489
    • Smirnov, E., & Milić Žitnik, I. (2025). High-order mean-motion resonances in the main belt. Astronomy & Astrophysics, 703, A237. doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202557400
