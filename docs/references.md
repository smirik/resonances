# References

The physics pages cite these works. Page and equation numbers refer to the printed pages.

**Resonant dynamics**

- Murray, C. D., & Dermott, S. F. (1999). *Solar System Dynamics*. Cambridge University Press.
  Used: co-orbital frequencies (eq. 3.151, p. 94; eqs. 3.178–3.179, p. 105), the disturbing function
  (eq. 6.13, p. 227), Lagrange's equation for $a$ (eq. 6.145, p. 251), secular vectors (eqs. 7.18–7.19, p. 277),
  the resonant angle and its rate (eq. 8.18, p. 329; eq. 8.22, p. 331), the pendulum model, its period and
  width (eqs. 8.45–8.58, pp. 335–338).
- Morbidelli, A. (2002). *Modern Celestial Mechanics: Aspects of Solar System Dynamics*. Taylor & Francis.
  Used: single-resonance normal forms and three-body terms at second order (§9.3, pp. 221–223), multiplets
  and modulated resonances (§9.4, pp. 224–226).
- Gallardo, T. (2006). Atlas of the mean motion resonances in the Solar System. *Icarus*, 184, 29–38.
  doi:[10.1016/j.icarus.2006.04.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2006.04.001). Used: the averaged disturbing
  function at fixed $\sigma$ (eqs. 1–6, p. 30) and the stable centres (§3).
- Nesvorný, D., & Morbidelli, A. (1998). Three-body mean motion resonances and the chaotic structure of the
  asteroid belt. *Astronomical Journal*, 116, 3029–3037. doi:[10.1086/300632](https://doi.org/10.1086/300632).
  Used: three-body angles (eqs. 1–2, p. 3029), filtering before decimation (§2, p. 3030), the strength scale
  of three-body resonances (§3, pp. 3031–3032).
- Nesvorný, D., & Morbidelli, A. (1998). An analytic model of three-body mean motion resonances.
  *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy*, 71, 243–271.
  doi:[10.1023/A:1008347020890](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1008347020890).
- Wisdom, J. (1985). A perturbative treatment of motion near the 3/1 commensurability. *Icarus*, 63, 272–289.
  doi:[10.1016/0019-1035(85)90011-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0019-1035(85)90011-9). Used: the action of the
  pendulum (§III, p. 277) and adiabatic changes of the well (pp. 274, 279).
- Henrard, J., & Lemaître, A. (1983). A second fundamental model for resonance. *Celestial Mechanics*, 30,
  197–218. doi:[10.1007/BF01234306](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01234306).
- Lemaître, A. (1984). High-order resonances in the restricted three-body problem. *Celestial Mechanics*, 32,
  109–126. doi:[10.1007/BF01231119](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01231119).
- Henrard, J. (1982). Capture into resonance: an extension of the use of adiabatic invariants.
  *Celestial Mechanics*, 27, 3–22.

- Li, M., Huang, Y., & Gong, S. (2019). Survey of asteroids in retrograde mean motion resonances with planets.
  *Astronomy & Astrophysics*, 630, A60. doi:[10.1051/0004-6361/201936117](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201936117).
  Used: the angle of a retrograde resonance.

**Lidov-Kozai**

- Lidov, M. L. (1962). The evolution of orbits of artificial satellites of planets under the action of
  gravitational perturbations of external bodies. *Planetary and Space Science*, 9, 719–759.
  doi:[10.1016/0032-0633(62)90129-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0032-0633(62)90129-0). Used: the two integrals
  $c_1, c_2$ (eqs. 58–60, p. 741).
- Kozai, Y. (1962). Secular perturbations of asteroids with high inclination and eccentricity.
  *Astronomical Journal*, 67, 591–598. doi:[10.1086/108790](https://doi.org/10.1086/108790). Used: the
  quadrupole energy integral (eqs. 24, 26–28, p. 594).
- Shevchenko, I. I. (2017). *The Lidov-Kozai Effect — Applications in Exoplanet Research and Dynamical
  Astronomy*. Astrophysics and Space Science Library 441, Springer. doi:[10.1007/978-3-319-43522-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-43522-0).
  Used: Lidov's integrals $c_1, c_2$ and the libration criterion (eqs. 3.35–3.36, pp. 35–36; eq. 3.43, p. 42),
  the quadrupole Hamiltonian in the Kinoshita–Nakai form (eq. 3.41, p. 41).

**Signal processing and statistics**

- Knežević, Z., & Milani, A. (2000). Synthetic proper elements for outer main belt asteroids.
  *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy*, 78, 17–46 (reprinted in Dvorak, R. & Henrard, J., eds.,
  *New Developments in the Dynamics of Planetary Systems*, Springer, 2001). Used: aliasing of a sampled angle and
  filtering before decimation (§2.2, pp. 22–23).
- Wilson, E. B. (1927). Probable inference, the law of succession, and statistical inference.
  *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 22, 209–212.
  doi:[10.1080/01621459.1927.10502953](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1927.10502953).
