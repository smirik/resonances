    Dry-run: check a run before integrating it¶

    A run of hours or days can be spoiled by its output step (an aliased angle, a filter that cannot see a short libration) or by its integrator (a fixed step through close encounters). The dry-run predicts this from the bodies' elements, before anything is integrated. It changes no verdict. The rules it applies are those of the analysis (From the integration to the angle: alias, cutoff, shortest libration).

    import resonances

sim = resonances.Simulation(name='dry-run-demo', integration_years=100_000, output_step='auto')
sim.create_solar_system()
sim.add_body(463, '4J-2S-1', name='463')  # or resonances.find(...) / resonances.check(...)
report = sim.dry_run(level='static')  # elements only: milliseconds per body
report = sim.dry_run(level='pilot')  # a short fine integration per body: 1-3 s each
report.summary()  # tables and warnings
rows, bodies, run = report.rows, report.bodies, report.run  # DataFrame body x MMR, DataFrame of bodies, dict of the run
report.apply()  # set output_step/Nout and the recommended integrator/dt on the config
report.to_csv('out')  # dryrun_rows.csv, dryrun_bodies.csv, dryrun_run.json

    Command line: resonances dry-run <arguments of find, or of check with --resonances> [--pilot] [--years N] [--step auto|YR] [--fair] [--out DIR]. resonances find and resonances check run the static level before integrating and write its warnings to the log; with OUTPUT_STEP=auto they set the step from it.

    What is checked (per MMR row)¶

    Notation: \(\sigma = \sum_j k_j\lambda_j + k\lambda + p\varpi + \sum_j p_j\varpi_j + v\Omega + \sum_j v_j\Omega_j\), \(\Delta t\) the output step, a in au, t in years (2π REBOUND units), rates in rad/yr. For a retrograde angle the body's terms are \(\lambda^* = M + \omega - \Omega\) and \(\varpi^* = \omega - \Omega\) (Resonance matrices). The static body precession is the Laplace-Lagrange free rate of a test particle (Murray & Dermott 1999, §7.9), \(\dot\varpi = A\), \(\dot\Omega = -A\),

    \[ A = \frac n4\sum_j \mu_j\,\alpha_j\bar\alpha_j\,b_{3/2}^{(1)}(\alpha_j), \qquad b_s^{(m)}(\alpha) = \frac1\pi\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{\cos m\psi\,{\rm d}\psi}{(1 - 2\alpha\cos\psi + \alpha^2)^s}, \]

    with \(\mu_j = m_j/M_\odot\), \(\alpha_j = \min(a, a_j)/\max(a, a_j)\), \(\bar\alpha_j = 1\) for an inner planet and \(\alpha_j\) for an outer one. It is omitted (taken as 0) for planet-crossers and retrograde orbits.

    check formula columns
    rate of σ \(\nu(a) = \sum_j k_j n_j + k\,n(a) + p\dot\varpi + \sum_j p_j g_j + v\dot\Omega + \sum_j v_j s_j\), \(n = 2\pi a^{-3/2}\); \(n_j\) of the package model (Murray & Dermott 1999, eq. 8.22); static \(\dot\varpi = A\), \(\dot\Omega = -A\) (above) rate_deg_yr, rate_min, rate_max over a_lo..a_hi
    alias branch \(m = \mathrm{round}(\nu\Delta t/2\pi)\) and apparent rate \(\nu - 2\pi m/\Delta t\) over a_lo..a_hi: resolved, marginal, aliased_unambiguous (one m ≠ 0: a true circulation) or ambiguous, by the analysis' rule turns_per_step, branches, verdict, apparent_deg_yr, expected_angle_status
    Nyquist half-width \(\delta a_N = a^{5/2}/(3\lvert k\rvert\Delta t)\), where \(\lvert\nu\rvert\Delta t = \pi\) nyquist_halfwidth_au
    ghosts the sampled angle stands still at \(\nu(a_m) = 2\pi m/\Delta t\) and imitates a resonance there ghost_axis_au, nearest_ghost_au
    safe steps plain unwrap ηπ/max|ν|; predictive unwrap η a^(5/2)/(3|k| δa_sp), η = MMR_STEP_RESOLVED_DEG/180° max_safe_step_plain, max_safe_step_pred, action
    perihelion jitter |p| δe_sp / e > MMR_JITTER_MAX_RAD jitter, jitter_flag
    cutoff the analysis' cutoff \(P_c\) from the longest synodic period of the angle's planets and the shortest libration it allows, MMR_LIBRATION_PERIOD_MIN_YR (a co-orbital: a share of its tadpole period); independent of \(\Delta t\) (rule) cutoff_pred_yr, cutoff_binding, samples_per_cutoff, libration_floor_yr
    validity \(P_c \le 2\Delta t\): the analysis returns invalid/undersampled (rule; Δt ≥ 37.5 yr, a Jupiter Trojan 18.5 yr); the resample step is the largest of MMR_RESAMPLE_STEPS_YR that gives MMR_RESAMPLE_STEPS_PER_CYCLE points per shortest libration within the alias bounds (Trojan 2.5 yr) step_valid_yr, expected_status, expected_reason, resample_step_yr, action
    short periods the step folds the orbital or a synodic period of the angle's planets, or one of their harmonics up to MMR_SHORT_PERIOD_HARMONICS slower than the period floor: the analysis flags short_period_aliased (the full tree, v2_verdict, also caps the confidence at medium there, except on a plain circulation) (rule). The row reads the analysis' own predicate (sampling.short_period_trigger) with P_orb(a₀), the analysis with P_orb(⟨a⟩); the pilot replans with the measured ⟨a⟩. The auto step avoids both cases step_short_period_yr, folded_slowest_yr, short_period_trigger (nyquist, harmonic), expected_flags
    shortest libration P_floor / MMR_RESAMPLE_STEPS_PER_CYCLE (20 points per cycle: 7.5 yr; Trojan 3.69 yr) step_libration_yr
    baseline (LIBRATION_MIN_TURNING_POINTS/2) × P_floor + 2 × FILTER_EDGE_TRIM_CUTOFFS × P_c baseline_min_yr, baseline_ok
    pilot only leak of short-period lines into the filtered angle and the filtered ȧ at Δt (decimate, unwrap, filter, compare with the fine record): σ largest deviation, ȧ relative rms error ε; the step is accepted when 2 × leak_σ ≤ the TP prominence of the fine σ_f (a wave of half-amplitude L turns by up to 2L) and ε ≤ √(b/(1−b)) = 0.33, b = CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND (a force law of R² = 1/(1+ε²) stays in the thin band); lines the filter passes, named |p n_j − q n| when they are near-commensurabilities leak_deg, leak_adot_rel, leak_tp_prominence_deg, pilot_step_ok, leak_slips, forced_terms

    action: none (resolved), predictive_unwrap (the analysis' unwrap recovers the angle), resample (it cannot, or the step is too coarse for the filter: integrate again with a smaller step), drop (only with DRYRUN_DROP_ALIASED: a guaranteed aliased circulation more than DRYRUN_DROP_GHOST_FACTOR × δa_N from a*).

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai rows¶

    Advisory and static (also at the pilot level): warnings only, no step and no verdict change. Table long_period (file dryrun_long_period.csv), one row per secular or ZLK angle, with the analysis' own rules (secular signal):

    check formula columns
    cutoff \(P_c\) of choose_cutoff: the great inequality, the shortest libration and, for a secular angle of two or more modes, its forced waves \(2\pi/\lvert f - f_j\rvert\) with the static \(g = A\), \(s = -A\) (none for crossers and retrograde orbits: forced_waves = unknown, the analysis' cutoff may then be longer) cutoff_pred_yr, cutoff_binding, forced_waves, g_static_arcsec_yr
    baseline N = (LIBRATION_MIN_TURNING_POINTS/2) × P_floor + 2 × FILTER_EDGE_TRIM_CUTOFFS × P_c, P_floor = max(the type's shortest libration, FILTER_MIN_LIBRATION_CUTOFFS × P_c): secular 1.5 × 200 + 2 × 100 = 500 kyr, ZLK 1.5 × 3748 + 2 × 1874 = 9370 yr at the defaults libration_floor_yr, floor_setting, baseline_min_yr
    free-ω gate (ZLK, gate on) FREE_GATE_MIN_CYCLES × τ, τ the quadrupole ZLK time \(1/\sum_j \tau_j^{-1}\), \(\tau_j = \frac{2}{3\pi}\frac{M_\odot}{m_j}\frac{P_j^2}{P}(1-e_j^2)^{3/2}\) over the planets outside the orbit (Antognini 2015, eq. 27; 153 Hilda: τ = 3.8 kyr, 11.3 kyr for the gate). Optimistic: a libration lasts several τ zlk_time_yr, gate_min_yr
    record span ≥ max(baseline, gate) required_yr, baseline_ok
    step Δt < P_c/2, else the filter cannot be built (uncertain / unusable_series) step_valid_yr, step_ok

    A 100-kyr run of a secular angle warns span 100000 yr < 500000 yr …: no libration can be confirmed there, and the verdict will be uncertain / baseline_needed with a next check of 400 kyr (How long to extend).

    Per body¶

    class (main, nea for q < DRYRUN_NEA_Q, crosser when [q, Q] overlaps a planet's [q_j − h R_H, Q_j + h R_H], h = DRYRUN_HILL_MARGIN), integrator_recommended and dt_recommended_yr: ias15 for crossers and NEAs (adaptive steps for encounters), otherwise SABA(10,6,4) with dt = min(run dt, P/DRYRUN_STEPS_PER_ORBIT, τ_peri/DRYRUN_STEPS_PER_PERI), τ_peri = P√((1−e)³/(1+e)) the effective period at pericentre (Lu, Hernandez & Rein 2024, eq. 33; the /10 rule is provisional). fair_step_yr = DRYRUN_FAIR_FRACTION × P; the pilot adds moid_<planet> (a grid screen at the epoch: information, nodes precess), the measured ⟨a⟩, δa_sp, δe_sp, ϖ̇, n from λ and its wall time. A crosser's a jumps at encounters, so its static alias verdict holds only for a₀ ± DRYRUN_DA_STATIC (it says so).

    The run and OUTPUT_STEP=auto¶

    Δt_auto = min(libration, short_period, filter, trend, FAIR, user) (short_period and filter are exclusive bounds), rounded down to DRYRUN_AUTO_STEPS_YR, then walked down DRYRUN_AUTO_STEPS_YR to the first step at which no row folds a harmonic slower than its period floor (binding_rule short_period_harmonic when it moved, step_fold_free_yr in the run; when no step of the grid is fold-free the rounded step stays and its harmonic rows keep short_period_aliased: P_orb 10.001 yr gives short_period 5.0005 yr, but 5 yr folds 2/P_orb to 50 kyr; 2.5 yr folds nothing); Nout = ⌈span/Δt⌉ + 1 (at least 2), so the grid step never exceeds Δt. short_period is the smallest min(P_orb, P_syn)/2 of the rows, trend the smallest predictive safe step, FAIR counts only with DRYRUN_FAIR=required (or --fair), user is DRYRUN_MAX_STEP_YR. With the default settings the short periods decide in the main belt (a = 2.5 au: 1 yr; a Hilda: 2.5 yr) and for NEAs (1999 UQ: 0.5 yr), the libration bound for a Jupiter Trojan (2.5 yr). After a run, each record's own fold-free step is in smp_fold_free_step_yr (the same walk for that row alone on MMR_RESAMPLE_STEPS_YR, see output.md), and action=resample names it when the folded periods withdraw a check the level needs (R-ALIAS-A, rsp_short_period_unmeasured). auto is defined only for runs with MMRs; a purely secular or ZLK run keeps Nout. A numeric OUTPUT_STEP sets Nout = ⌈span/step⌉ + 1; an explicit Nout overrides both. The default step is unchanged (15.9 yr): a Hilda keeps P_c = 75 yr there and its libration, but every main-belt row is flagged short_period_aliased (the full tree, v2_verdict, caps it at medium; the final level drops only where a check is withdrawn). The run warns of each limit the configured step is above; the pilot lifts the short-period warning when it accepts the step for every flagged row (153 Hilda and the Trojans: leaks 0.1–0.7°, ε 0.1–0.2), and keeps it when not (62243 1M-4J-1: 2.4°, ε 5.9). A row below that step whose harmonic folds slower than the floor and whose leak the pilot rejects adds the short_period_harmonic limit, with the fold-free step to use instead. The analysis does not read the pilot: the cap stays on every such row except a plain circulation.

    The run also gets the recommended integrator (ias15 if any body needs it; mixed_integrators then suggests separate runs), the cost (rows, disk_GB if every body is saved, ram_per_worker_GB, cpu_hours; coefficients measured in 2026-10, the pilot's own speed when present) and step_spread.

    Examples¶

    • 1999 UQ 7E+2M-9, ias15, 20 kyr, Nout 4000 (5.0012 yr). Static: aliased_unambiguous, 0.994 turns per step, ghost 1.0941756 au, 1.2·10⁻⁴ au from a₀: the "libration" of the record was a stroboscope. The pilot puts ⟨a⟩ 2·10⁻⁶ au from the ghost, gives 71.98°/yr (3999 turns in 20 kyr, as the 1-yr rerun) and finds the real 97-yr forced wave 7n_E − 8n (9.6°). At 1 yr the angle is resolved (0.2 turns per step).
    • 153 Hilda 3J-2, SABA, 100 kyr. Class main, SABA with dt = 58 d. Pilot: the libration line at 273 yr (the 1-yr record: 268 yr).
    • 1865 Cerberus. Crosses Venus, Earth and Mars: ias15; a SABA run is flagged.

    On the 294 benchmark MMR records whose metadata has the initial elements, the static verdict at each record's own step against the verdict measured on the record: 286 agree; every record that the static level calls not resolved is not resolved; 3 records are ambiguous but statically resolved — all crossers whose a moved by 0.03-0.5 au over 20-500 kyr (99942, 2000 YG4, 2020 PP1).
