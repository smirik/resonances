    Free elements and the free-omega gate¶

    An osculating element vector is the sum of a forced part, imposed by the planets and turning at their frequencies, and a free part carrying the object's own secular motion. Splitting the two answers a question the osculating angle cannot: does ω really librate, or does it only look bounded because the forced eccentricity offsets the cloud?

    For a Lidov-Kozai resonance this is part of the decision: the free-ω gate below is a step of the ZLK analysis, and its outcome enters the status (Secular and Lidov-Kozai). The cross spectra decide nothing (Cross-spectral coherence).

    What is computed¶

    Work happens in the non-singular variables (the same ones secular/proper_angle.py builds), because vectors add and angles do not:

    \[ z_e(t) = e\,e^{{\rm i}\varpi} \ \ (\text{driven by } g_5 \ldots g_8), \qquad z_i(t) = \sin\tfrac{i}{2}\,e^{{\rm i}\Omega} \ \ (\text{driven by } s_5 \ldots s_8). \]

    The forced part is removed by a complex least-squares fit at the known planetary frequencies from resonances/data/const.py. Fitting known frequencies rather than searching for them is deliberate: a frequency analysis (NAFF/FMFT) of a real integration splits the free mode into five or six neighboring terms — on 4257 the spread is ±0.5 ″/yr against a Rayleigh resolution of 0.13 — and any of those splinters can be mistaken for a planetary line and subtracted by accident.

    What is left gives

    \[ \varpi_{\rm free} = \arg z_{e,\rm free}, \qquad \Omega_{\rm free} = \arg z_{i,\rm free}, \qquad \omega_{\rm free} = \varpi_{\rm free} - \Omega_{\rm free}, \]

    with their least-squares rates b_varpi, b_Omega, b_omega in ″/yr.

    Which modes can be fitted¶

    Not all of them, and the rule is pairwise, not per-mode. The Rayleigh width is \(1/T\) cycles/yr (\(1\,296\,000/T\) ″/yr): two modes closer than that never drift a full turn apart over the window, so no fit can tell them apart and least squares splits their amplitude arbitrarily. To be conservative, candidates are grouped by single linkage at twice that width, \(2/T\) (two relative turns over the window), and one member of each group is fitted. The gate's tolerance (below) is one width.

    At 500 kyr the two widths are 2.592 and 5.184 ″/yr; the grouping threshold 5.18 ″/yr and the candidate rates 0, 0.67, 3.09, 4.26, 28.25 have neighbor gaps 0.67, 2.42, 1.17, 23.99 — the first three chain together, the last one breaks:

    baseline z_e basis z_i basis
    100 kyr one line one line
    300 kyr – 1 Myr g5, g6 const, s6
    10 Myr full full

    The representative is a modeling assumption¶

    Which member of a cluster gets fitted is fixed by a documented priority — g5 > g6 > g7 > g8 > const for the eccentricity vector, const > s6 > s7 > s8 for the inclination one. That order is not a free convention. Fitting a different member measurably changes the verdict: on 3752 the gate's outcome moves from confirmed to no verdict, on 591986 from kinematic to no verdict. The order encodes what actually dominates the forcing — Jupiter's g5 for a main-belt eccentricity vector, and the constant offset between the ecliptic and the invariable plane (the s5 mode, zero by construction) for the slow inclination modes — and it reproduces the {g5, g6} / {const, s6} basis that was validated independently against 10 Myr integrations.

    What makes it safe rather than merely plausible: no choice of representative ever turns a non-librator into a libration. Errors move the answer towards withholding a verdict, never towards inventing one. That is asserted directly in the tests.

    Resampling¶

    Before the fit both vectors are thinned to about free_elements_sampling_years (500 yr) by the integer factor \(q = {\rm round}(500\ {\rm yr}/\Delta t)\), with an anti-alias filter on the real and imaginary parts: secular work needs nothing finer, and a short-period term folded into the secular band would show up as free motion. Every factor is filtered. Passes of at most 10 use scipy's zero-phase FIR decimate; what no such pass divides (a prime above 10: \(q = 31\) at the 15.918-yr step of the 100-kyr ZLK records) is a last polyphase pass (resample_poly, padded along the end-to-end line). The grid is times[::q]. Earlier versions skipped that leftover and the record kept its 15.9-yr grid: Jupiter's 11.86-yr term aliased to ~47 yr and, where it was larger than the free circle, wound ϖ_free by whole turns (555527: 26 turns of excess in one 2-kyr window; 0.37 after the filter, and the gate goes from kinematic to planetary_residual). A row resampled with a polyphase pass carries the rule free_resample_remainder in rules_fired.

    The mask¶

    Where |z_free| passes near the origin the argument is undefined, and unwrapping through it picks up half-turns that look like circulation. Samples below mask_quantile × median are dropped before unwrapping. ϖ_free and Ω_free each live on their own mask; ω_free lives on the intersection, so |b_omega − (b_varpi − b_Omega)| is a consistency check with a tolerance, not an identity. In practice the masks remove 0–8% of a real series.

    The gate¶

    The gate (omega_free_gate.evaluate_gate) reads the free elements and returns an outcome:

    condition outcome
    no free elements (fewer than 16 samples after decimation, \(T \lesssim 8\) kyr, or the split failed) unavailable
    a co-orbital of a planet whose own modes are not fitted: \(\lvert\langle a\rangle/a_P - 1\rvert < k\,(m_P/3M_\odot)^{1/3}\) (ZLK_COORBITAL_HILL_FACTOR \(k = 3\): 0.030 for the Earth), \(P\) = Mercury … Mars unavailable (zlk_gate_detail = coorbital_unfitted_forcing)
    \(T <\) FREE_GATE_MIN_CYCLES × \(P_{\rm lib}\): the window never held the libration short_window
    more than half the samples masked: no usable argument undefined_phase
    \(R < 360°\) and b_varpi at g5…g8 (or 0, see below) or b_Omega at s6…s8 planetary_residual
    \(R < 360°\) otherwise confirmed
    \(R \ge 360°\) and b_varpi at g5…g8, b_Omega at s6…s8, or b_omega at any \(g_j - s_k\) (\(j = 5…8\), \(k = 5…8\), \(s_5 = 0\)) planetary_residual
    \(R \ge 360°\) otherwise: the free ω winds kinematic
    FREE_GATE_ENABLED=False disabled
    the osculating ω is not bounded (it circulates): no confirmation asked not_applicable

    "At" means a signed difference below one Rayleigh width, \(1\,296\,000/T\) ″/yr. The difference frequencies \(g_j - s_k\) are tested on the winding branch only: \(\omega = \varpi - \Omega\), so an unsubtracted term in either vector shows up in \(\omega\) at such a frequency even when neither b_varpi nor b_Omega matches a mode. not_applicable is not a failed split; the geometric status stands.

    \(R\) is the span of \(\omega_{\rm free}\) trimmed by half a percent at each end (a span is an extreme statistic, and one surviving glitch ruins a strict maximum). The 360° threshold is topological and must not be tightened: inside a mean-motion resonance the Lidov-Kozai cycle is asymmetric and its center shifts away from 90°/270°, to 0°/180° for Hildas and by ±60° for Trojans (Vinogradova 2024).

    The status decision (analysis.secular.decide.decide_status) turns the outcome into the status: on a librating ω, confirmed (or disabled) keeps resonant, kinematic gives non_resonant / kinematic_libration and any other outcome uncertain / gate_unavailable; on episodes, a drift or too few cycles only kinematic with \(\rho_{\max} < 1\) changes it (\(\rho_{\max}\) = the largest ratio of the free to the forced radius, each a rolling median over about one libration cycle, FreeElements.rho_local_max). The table and \(\rho_{\max}\): Secular and Lidov-Kozai. For screening, uncertain means "re-check on a longer baseline", so the candidates and extended save/plot modes keep it; extend_to_years says how long (short_window: the missing cycles; planetary_residual: the baseline that resolves the residual's distance to the planetary rate, zlk_gate_d_min_arcsec_yr), and undefined_phase or unavailable asks for review (How long to extend). The gate itself has no amplitude test (below).

    Why the same check stands on both branches¶

    A Lidov-Kozai libration lives on the object's own rates, so it can only be confirmed when ϖ_free and Ω_free turn at speeds that are not planetary. A residual sitting at a planetary rate is either a defect of the subtraction or a genuine linear secular resonance (g near some g_j, the ν5/ν6 family) where the forced/free split is undefined to begin with. Both make a verdict unsafe, and telling them apart is not required.

    Comparison is signed and the tolerance is one Rayleigh width, 1296000/T ″/yr. Signed because every s_j is retrograde: a prograde free node is not s8 however close the magnitudes are. Zero is a target only for the eccentricity vector, and only when the constant term was clustered away and so left in the residual — an unsubtracted constant is exactly what makes an angle look confined.

    The honest cost: an object whose own g or s sits within one Rayleigh width of a planetary fundamental is withheld rather than confirmed. That is a real ambiguity, not a gap.

    The free node near s_j. When the only match is the free node (b_Omega within one width of s_j, b_varpi and, on a winding ω, b_omega away from every planetary rate), the column zlk_gate_node_near names s_j, with zlk_gate_node_near_d_arcsec_yr (\(d\)), zlk_gate_node_near_resolution_arcsec_yr and zlk_gate_node_separation_yr = \(1\,296\,000/d\), the free-element baseline whose width is \(d\). 1981 Midas over 500 kyr: ω librates 19 cycles (the gate needs 3), but the free node is \(d = 1.85\)″/yr from s6 against a width of 2.59″/yr; it separates at 0.70 Myr, and the extension asks for 1 Myr (ZLK_EXTEND_RESOLUTION_MARGIN). The status stays uncertain / gate_unavailable.

    free_rho_i_max is the inclination's counterpart of \(\rho_{\max}\): the largest rolling ratio of \(|z_{i,\rm free}|\) to the turning forced inclination (the fitted s modes without the constant, which is the tilt of the reference plane; empty when the baseline merged s6…s8 into the constant, 373136 over 100 kyr). Far above one, a free node near s_j could not be a leak of the forced s_j term, which is at most as large as that term. But while \(d\) is below the width, least squares splits the free node between the residual and the s_j term, so the ratio is not yet the physical one (1981: the fitted s6 term 0.30 against a free radius 0.25, ratio 0.5–1.4). No status reads it; a rule on it waits for the 1-Myr run, and a true ν16 lock (s held at s6) is not separated by any length.

    No amplitude criterion in the gate¶

    An earlier design compared the free eccentricity against an estimated floor of unsubtracted forcing. That floor cannot be measured: estimating how much forcing sits at a frequency the baseline cannot resolve requires resolving it. Measured on the reference set, a ridge prefit of the candidate amplitudes returned |ĉ| of order e_free rather than e_forced — two orders of magnitude too large — because the near-degenerate slow cluster amplifies the projection of the un-modeled free signal. The floor then scaled with the very quantity it was being compared against, and demoted 3040, 162474 and 3752 outright.

    Frequency is the direct observable instead: unsubtracted forcing lives, by construction, only at known rates.

    Where the method stops working¶

    For a co-orbital the forced term is the perturber's own eccentricity vector rather than a planetary fundamental. For a Jupiter Trojan that vector is Jupiter's, g5 + g6, rotated by ±60° for L4/L5 (Milani 1993; Vinogradova 2015), and the split fits it: on 591986 the fitted g5 amplitude is 1.02 × Jupiter's own g5 term (g6: 1.12 ×) and the forced direction swings ±arcsin(|c₆|/|c₅|) = ±22.8° at the g6 − g5 beat, which is the slow wave of the osculating ω's center. So the kinematic outcome stands (Vinogradova 2024 finds no Trojan with a librating ω); the tadpole sidebands are not modeled, and the free rates there are not proper elements.

    A co-orbital of Mercury to Mars is different: those planets' own modes (g1–g4, s1–s4) are not candidates of the split, so its forced term is simply not subtracted. Such a body (inside ZLK_COORBITAL_HILL_FACTOR Hill radii of the planet's axis) gets the outcome unavailable, detail coorbital_unfitted_forcing: a libration becomes uncertain / gate_unavailable, and so does an episodic row, a drift or too few cycles (2020 PP1, an Earth co-orbital, and 10563 over 500 kyr; rule zlk_coorbital_episodic): no record length makes the split fit, so the action is review. Never not_applicable, which would keep the geometric status of a libration.

    An episodic \(\omega\) circulates between its episodes, so its free \(\omega\) winds over the record; where the record's outcome is kinematic or a winding planetary_residual, the gate reads the librating episodes instead (ZLK_GATE_EPISODES): confirmed (detail episodes) when in every one the free vector is the longer and the free \(\omega\) advances less than half a turn per cycle, with \(b_\varpi\), \(b_\Omega\) off the modes (the gate inside the episodes).

    Any other episodic row without a verdict (a planetary residual, a short window, an undefined phase) keeps the geometry's status under the minimal policy (flag gate_unavailable, at most medium); the full tree (v2_verdict) made it transient only when \(e\) and \(i\) trade at constant \(\sqrt{1-e^2}\cos i\) inside its deciding episode (zlk_ei_episode_coupling = zlk), else uncertain / gate_unavailable: From the outcome to the status.

    Beyond the gate¶

    Columns of a ZLK row that change no status (analysis.zlk.diagnostics, analysis.secular.slow):

    • The e–i coupling at the decisive period \(P\) (\(P_{\rm cyc}\); for a circulating ω the \(\cos 2\omega\) harmonic \(\pi/|\dot\omega|\)), for every row whatever its status. One sinusoid of \(e\) and one of \(i\) (from the invariable plane by an exact rotation; the tilt \(\min(i, 180° - i)\) of a retrograde orbit; an orbit that crosses 90° is not_measured) on the record less one period at each edge, and \(e\), \(i\) band-passed to \([(1 \mp 0.2)/P]\) for their correlation \(r\). Two laws predict \(\delta i/\delta e\): constant \(\sqrt{1-e^2}\cos i\) (ZLK, \(-e\cot i/(1-e^2)\), antiphase) and constant \(\sqrt{1-e^2}(1-\cos i)\) (the momentum conjugate to Ω when the forcing acts on ϖ only, as a forced-eccentricity beat does: \(+e\tan(i/2)/(1-e^2)\), in phase). zlk_ei_coupling is zlk or forced_beat when the phase is within ZLK_EI_PHASE_TOL_DEG of the law's sign, \(r\) has that sign with \(|r| \ge\) ZLK_EI_MIN_BAND_R and \(|\delta i|/|\delta e|\) is within a factor ZLK_EI_AMPLITUDE_FACTOR of the law; none when every number is finite and neither law matches. A band the sampling cannot hold (above the Nyquist frequency: undersampled), one the filter cannot pass (band_unmeasured) or a law that is not finite (law_undefined) is not_measured (zlk_ei_reason), never none. The librators 3040, 162474, 4257, 1373, 1981: zlk, phase 177–182°, \(r \approx -0.995\), 1.0–1.6 × the law. 591986's 6-kyr cycle: forced_beat, phase 328°, \(\delta i\) 1.2 × the beat law. Coupling is not libration: 15527 circulates and shows zlk coupling at its 2.05-kyr harmonic. The deciding episode's own coupling (zlk_ei_episode_*: demodulated on the episode's samples, with the residual \(\eta_H\) of the integral) is the one the status of an episodic row without a gate verdict reads; on 88899 over 500 kyr the record says none and so does the episode, on 2020 PP1 the record says zlk and the deciding episode none.
    • Attribution of a kinematic libration: the fitted mode with the largest \(|c_j|\) (named with the modes the baseline merged into it) holds ϖ, provided the free circle stays off the origin (\(\rho_{\max} < 1\), or the strict pin \(\max_t|z_{\rm free}| + \sum_{k \ne j}|c_k| < |c_j|\): the largest free radius of the record, not its median, since the bound must hold at every sample), the predicted beat \(2\pi/|b_\varpi - g_j|\) is \(P_{\rm cyc}\) within a Rayleigh width, and the free node follows the mode (\(|b_\Omega - g_j|\,T\) under one turn). 591986: g5 (g5+g7+g8+const), \(e_{\rm free}/|c_5| = 0.25\), beat 5.96 kyr against \(P_{\rm cyc}\) 6.0 kyr, node − g5 = −0.17″/yr (0.06 turn). Otherwise "forcing not attributed" with the condition that failed. An episodic \(\omega\) that the gate demoted inside its episodes (median local \(\rho < 1\) in each, the free \(\omega\) turning with each cycle) is attributed with zlk_attribution_scope episodes: the node need not follow the mode there (624 Hektor: node − g5 = −17.2″/yr, 6.6 turns of the center \(\varpi_5 - \Omega\), which keeps the librations apart). The per-mode amplitudes are persisted in {body}-omega-free.csv, so a restored run gets its attribution back.
    • Node lock (zlk_node_lock, a flag no status reads): the osculating node against the longitude of the fitted mode with the nearest rate, \(\varpi_j(t) = g_j t + \arg c_j\). Locked when \(|b_\Omega - g_j| < 1296000/T\) (one Rayleigh width), \(\Omega - \varpi_j\) stays within a turn, and, low-passed at the node's longest forced wave (\(2\pi/|b_\Omega - s_k|\)) inside the filter's edge trim, it closes at least ZLK_NODE_LOCK_MIN_CYCLES full cycles ((turning points − 1)/2): a drift or a slow circulation within the resolution closes none. 591986 over 4 Myr: g5, 50–130°, 2 cycles of 1.4 Myr; over 500 kyr the rate matches and \(\Omega - \varpi_5\) stays within 52–91°, but no cycle closes: unresolved (one period of 1.4 Myr does not fit). Columns: the mode, the reason when none (rate_mismatch, unbounded, unresolved, …), the candidate, \(b_\Omega - g_j\) and the resolution, the range, the period and the cycles.
    • The slow line of the center of a libration or a drift (zlk_centre_*): the strongest sinusoid of the center between its smoothing \(W_c\) and half the record, and its origin: forced_beat:<gk>-<gj> when its period is a beat \(2\pi/|g_k - g_j|\) of two fitted forced modes with nonzero amplitudes within a Rayleigh width and the phase wave of the fitted forced vector, smoothed like the center, explains it in shape (\(r \ge\) ZLK_CENTRE_MODEL_R_MIN) and in size (zlk_centre_model_gain, its swing over the center's, within a factor ZLK_CENTRE_MODEL_AMP_FACTOR: correlation alone is blind to size), node when the node's own wave does, forced_direction when \(\arg z_{\rm forced} - \Omega\) does (the center of \(\omega\) itself when the forced vector holds \(\varpi\): 591986 over 4 Myr, a 1.43-Myr wave of the node locked to g5 plus the g6−g5 beat, \(r = 0.998\); the node alone gives 0.82), else unexplained. Fewer than two nonzero per-mode amplitudes: no forced beat. 591986: 53.8 kyr, ±21.6°, forced_beat:g6-g5, \(r = 0.991\), gain 1.00, model ±22.8°. A modulated secular angle gets the same line (sec_centre_*), named forced_wave:<mode> after a forced wave of its own modes.
    • The scale guard: a libration at the center scale whose period is such a forced beat, whose forced phase wave explains the center (as above), while \(\rho_{\max} < 1\) never becomes the decisive libration (sec_centre_rejected); the gate's period stays the fast one (a clean synthetic 591986 otherwise decided at 54 kyr instead of 5.95 kyr). It acts only where the gate does: a disabled gate, no split, or a co-orbital with unfitted forcing keeps the geometric scale.

    Reference values¶

    Measured on 500 kyr of mercurius integrations at 100 yr, the baseline the gate is calibrated for:

    object outcome b_varpi b_Omega b_omega R, deg rho
    4257 confirmed −19.08 −19.05 −0.02 34 26.5
    3040 confirmed −21.27 −21.30 0.02 63 9.7
    162474 confirmed −15.55 −15.55 0.00 55 12.1
    3752 confirmed −13.81 −15.60 1.79 352 6.4
    591986 kinematic 221.62 4.09 217.53 29896 0.23

    The other five reference objects (373136, 797204, 88899, 555527, 10563) do not librate over the whole 500 kyr record; there the gate changes the status only on a kinematic outcome with \(\rho_{\max} < 1\).

    How long an integration is needed¶

    baseline librating and confirmed 591986 false positives
    300 kyr 4257, 3040, 162474, 3752 kinematic 88899 librates, gate: no verdict
    500 kyr 4257, 3040, 162474, 3752 kinematic none
    1 Myr 4257, 3040, 162474 kinematic none
    10 Myr 4257 kinematic none

    Longer is not better here. Most of these objects lose the lock well before 10 Myr — 3752 at 850 kyr, 162474 at 1.33 Myr, 3040 at 2.83 Myr — so a long baseline answers "does it stay?" rather than "is it in resonance?". 500 kyr recovers librators that 10 Myr loses while keeping every false positive out.

    Two things a 500 kyr run does not buy. The rates b_varpi, b_Omega cannot be quoted as numbers: the Rayleigh resolution there is 2.59 ″/yr, and the Lidov-Kozai signature g − s = 0.09 ″/yr on 4257 sits thirty times below it. The verdict is safe because it comes from the winding, not the rate; for numbers use 2 Myr (0.65 ″/yr) or 10 Myr (0.13). And the calibration assumes Lidov-Kozai cycles of 27–58 kyr, as in this set — the rule of thumb is \(T \ge \max(500\ {\rm kyr},\ 10\,P_{\rm ZLK})\), which in practice means a short run to measure the period and an extension if it turns out long.

    Columns in summary.csv¶

    Written for Lidov-Kozai rows only, since ω = ϖ − Ω is the angle they describe.

    The decomposition: free_basis_e, free_basis_i, free_e, free_e_forced, free_rho, free_rho_min, free_rho_max (the median free radius over the largest and the smallest smoothed forced amplitude; descriptive, the status reads zlk_gate_rho_max), free_omega_range_deg, free_omega_revolutions, free_b_varpi, free_b_Omega, free_b_omega, free_drift_flag, free_mask_e, free_mask_i, free_resolution.

    The gate: free_gate (the outcome), free_n_cycles (\(T/P_{\rm lib}\)), free_gate_matches (the planetary rates a residual could not be told apart from), and the same as the analysis read them: zlk_gate, zlk_gate_n_cycles (\(T/P_{\rm cyc}\): cycles of the osculating ω, not turns of the free one), zlk_gate_min_cycles, zlk_gate_period_yr, zlk_gate_matches, zlk_gate_detail (coorbital_unfitted_forcing), zlk_gate_rho_max (the local ratio the status reads), zlk_gate_rho_global (the former median ratio, descriptive), free_rho_i_max and zlk_gate_node_near* (the free node near s_j, numbers only). The check check_zlk_free_omega is pass (confirmed), fail (kinematic) or not_measured. The columns of Beyond the gate are listed in Output.

    Two notes on reading them. free_e / free_e_forced come from this fit, while the older e_free / e_forced columns come from the (k, h) scatter in cross_spectrum and exist for every resonance type — different estimators, expected to differ. And free_rho is an annotation (the status reads only \(\rho_{\max}\), Secular and Lidov-Kozai): for a large-amplitude librator e_forced is inflated by the same free signal the fit could not represent, so read it in the small-amplitude regime.

    free_drift_flag marks a span under a full turn that nonetheless carries a reproducible monotonic ramp — worth a longer integration, but not a demotion.

    Files and figures¶

    {body}-omega-free.csv carries the series themselves — the free and forced amplitudes of both vectors, the three unwrapped angles, and the two masks. Masked samples are NaN rather than dropped, so the file stays aligned with its times column. The fitted complex amplitude of every mode is in amp_<e|i>_<mode>_re / _im on the first row (its value at \(t = 0\) of the times column), so a restored run rebuilds the forced vectors with their phases.

    Add free_omega to PLOTS (or plots=[...]) for three figures (How to read them):

    • …-free-omega-drift.png — ω accumulated over the baseline, free against osculating;
    • …-free-omega-portrait.png — the Kozai diagram in free variables, free e against free ω;
    • …-free-omega-vector.png — the free eccentricity vector, on the same axes as the osculating ecc_vector figure of the same body: side by side they show whether the ring bounding ω is the object's own or the forced offset.

    No figure carries numbers. Read free_gate and the rates from summary.csv.

    Using it directly¶

    from resonances.resonance.omega_free_gate import evaluate_gate  # noqa: E402

elements = body.build_free_elements()
gate = evaluate_gate(elements, libration_period_yr, config)  # an outcome: confirmed, kinematic, or no verdict
print(gate.outcome, elements.b_omega)

    config is sim.config of the run, libration_period_yr the period of the bounded ω in years.

    In a run the gate is a step of analyse_zlk (resonances/resonance/analysis/zlk/), and analysis.secular.decide.decide_status turns its outcome into the status (above).

    Or on bare arrays, without a Body:

    from resonances.secular.free_elements import free_elements  # noqa: E402

elements = free_elements(times_years, ecc, inc, Omega, omega)

    Times in years, angles in radians.
