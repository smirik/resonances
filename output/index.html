    Output data¶

    A run writes into save_path (see Serialization for simulation.json and restore, Config for what is saved): the tables below and simulation.json at its top, the series (data-*, *-omega-free.csv) in data/ (data_folder), the figures in plot_path (see Layout). Which bodies get data files and figures is set by save and plot (status filters); summary.csv, episodes.csv and the other tables hold every body (save_summary). List cells are ;-separated; an empty cell means not measured, never 0. A unit is in the column name (_yr, _kyr, _deg, _rad, _au, _deg_yr, _rad_kyr, _arcsec_yr).

    file rows content
    summary.csv body × resonance the verdict, the checks and margins, the numbers the decision read
    mmr_numbers.csv body × MMR every number of the MMR analysis
    episodes.csv run of turning points accepted and rejected episodes of every resonance type (save_episodes)
    candidates.csv candidate subresonances and owners of the MMR analysis (save_candidates: top or all)
    constants.csv planet / term the run's planetary constants (save_constants)
    coherence.csv coherent line cross spectra (coherence)
    clones.csv body × resonance status shares of a body's clones with Wilson 95 % intervals (clones)
    data/data-<body>.csv time elements and angles of a saved body
    data/data-<body>-periodograms.csv frequency Lomb-Scargle periodograms of a and the angles
    data/data-planet-<planet>.csv time the planets save_planets keeps (all, analysis, none)
    data/<body>-omega-free.csv time forced/free series of a Lidov-Kozai body (free elements)
    data/ensemble-<body>.csv, .json clone every draw of a body's clones and its orbit solution (clones)
    data/ensemble-<body>-results.npz clone the clones' verdicts and thinned angles, read by restore (clones)

    Layout¶

    The folder names and the figures' subfolders are settings (Config: data_folder, plot_figures_folder, plot_combined_folder, plot_layout, plot_combined_layout; resonances/simulation/layout.py). With the defaults (save_path = plot_path = cache/<name>, data, figures, combined, plot_layout='confidence/status', the benchmark's tree) each figure goes into a subfolder per confidence level and status:

    cache/<name>/
  simulation.json  summary.csv  mmr_numbers.csv  episodes.csv  candidates.csv  constants.csv  coherence.csv
  data/        data-463.csv  data-463-periodograms.csv  data-planet-Jupiter.csv  463-omega-free.csv
  figures/     high/resonant/463-4J-2S-1+0+0-1-portrait.png   none/uncertain/490-...png  ...
  combined/    high/resonant/463-4J-2S-1+0+0-1-combined.png   medium/transient/...       ...

    The other presets are flat, status, confidence, status/confidence, type/status, type/confidence/status, resonance, body. With plot_layout='flat' the figures lie directly in their folders:

    cache/<name>/
  ...
  figures/     463-4J-2S-1+0+0-1.png  463-4J-2S-1+0+0-1-portrait.png  463-ecc-vector.png  ...
  combined/    463-4J-2S-1+0+0-1-combined.png  463-LK-combined.png  ...

    An empty folder name puts its files at the top (data_folder=''); a verdict without a confidence level (uncertain, invalid) goes to none, a row without a verdict to unclassified. The data files are per body, the status per body × resonance, so the data folder has no layout of its own. The benchmark sorts its figures with the same function (benchmark/tools/sort_figures.py).

    Batch workers append to the same tables; each write holds <table>.lock next to the table, and its holder removes it, so a finished run leaves no *.lock file (one left by a killed worker is reused by the next write). A second run into a folder that a run is still writing is refused (.run.lock). Both locks are flocks: on Windows, which has none, neither is taken, so do not start two runs into one folder there.

    Flags¶

    flags lists marks that change no status by themselves (a cap with the same name is in caps). Most are explained with their rule in the rule index and the pages it links (search for the name); these are not:

    flag meaning
    exit_and_capture an episodic angle that left a libration and came back at least once, with its own force measured; names no mechanism
    subresonance_composite the accepted member does not dominate the multiplet's composite coefficient (Rouché test)
    subresonance_theory_disagrees the theoretical center of the member (test D) was measured and disagrees; confidence only
    subresonance_theory_crossing no theoretical center: the orbits can intersect
    subresonance_theory_round_off no theoretical center: its coefficients are below round-off
    subresonance_theory_range no theoretical center: \(e\) or \(e_P\) outside (0, 1), or an empty window
    owner_catalogue_ambiguous an owner candidate on catalog longitudes lies within the phase error of a threshold: undecided
    owner_outside_episode an accepted owner covers less than MMR_OWNER_COVER_MIN of an own episode of σ (F7): written in neighbours, not owner; origin from the tree
    owner_psi_too_fast an accepted owner in its libration regime covers an episode but its Ψ/u turns at least MMR_OWNER_PSI_TURNS_PER_CYCLE_MAX times per cycle (P2): it cannot make those cycles; written in neighbours
    action_step an accepted run has a step in its libration action (the energy of its cycles jumps) that cuts the record; the run is split there like at a jump
    rejected_candidate the angle had turning points, but the record ends in circulation and what it had does not count as a libration (a 1:1 leg at the start, a single half-libration, or too short a record): non-resonant / circulation
    policy_unknown_leaf the minimal policy has no rule for the tree's leaf, so the geometry's status stands; a bug to report
    uncovered_bounded_run 1:1 coverage audit: the orbit-averaged angle holds a bounded run of at least MMR_COORBITAL_AUDIT_MIN_HALF_CYCLES half-cycles inside the window that no counted episode covers, and the local 1:1 law on it passes the co-orbital force check, not thin (aud_unc_*); a review reason, never a status. Clone rows of an ensemble do not carry it into PLOT=review (the clone archive keeps no flags)
    floor_above_own_loop 1:1 coverage audit: a period floor rejects a run although it exceeds the loop of the averaged potential through that run's own turns (an accepted run below its floor: the loop over its turn pairs, for a run of mixed turns that of its first pair; or a quasi-satellite pass rejected as below_period_floor, no_turning_points or no_run_in_well, which the floor causes through the pass's cutoff and prominence, whose stay's loop through ≥ 2 turns is below the QS floor; aud_floor_*); a review reason, never a status
    owner_unresolved no owner accepted; the best candidate's phases cannot separate it from σ and its potential route does not apply: check_mmr_owner not measured
    zone_eq_only the separatrix zone's modulation is the \(e^q\) estimate, not resolved multiplet members
    zone_edge_cycle at least one cycle lies within one σ of a zone edge: its class is not stable
    jump_locator_aliased the jump locator's time τ ≥ \(T_0/\pi\): the resonance's own swing can imitate a step of \(a\)
    extend_time MMR: a longer integration in extend_direction (forward, backward, both) would show how a record edge ends: an arc or stall after circulation at the end, the mirror at the start, one reflection cut by an edge, a clean 1:1 edge leg or an edge half-libration; extend_to_years holds its length; whatever the action, no status or level changes (the record's edges)
