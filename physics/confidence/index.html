    Confidence¶

    The status says what the record shows; the confidence says how close the record came to showing something else. It is built from the comparisons the analysis made on the way to the status, not from a statistical model of asteroid orbits. uncertain and invalid verdicts have no level: they claim nothing to be confident about.

    Minimal policy (default)¶

    The minimal policy's level is a pure function of five pieces of evidence (policy.level, column confidence_basis; policy.level_inputs, column pol_level_inputs, lists each input the level read with the highest level it allows, and the level is the lowest of them):

    • N, complete libration cycles: \((n_{TP} - 1)/2\) of the decisive episode, plus the gap of each covered edge in periods (a 2-TP run closed by its phase return counts 1, one 1:1 leg 0.5); a resonant row over several episodes sums them, an overlap counted once; a subresonance parent's N is its member's (N=…(member): no episode of its own);
    • M, the amplitude margin: L2 full (1:1: the zone known and no cycle in it; long-period: the largest half-amplitude below \((1 -\) CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND\()\) of the run break);
    • V, the vetoes: clean (the identity passes and the owner search found none, or does not apply; ZLK: the gate confirmed), partial (owner search not measured, a probable owner, W or the gate's cycles thin), not_measured (the identity or the gate gave no measurement; a failed identity on a step that turns only \(\varpi\) fast, flag varpi_fast_low_e, where \(\dot\sigma\) reads that \(\varpi\): 17346 2J-1), ambiguous (a measured objection, a failed onset, a failed identity that moved nothing, a kinematic gate that pins no episode);
    • S, filter stability: L1 full and no decisive number changing at the cutoff ×2 (flag filter_sensitive; long-period: F full and no filter_too_slow).
    • F (MMR), the angle's own force: shown when the force law on a (L4, the 1:1 law), the energy and period law (R0) or the strength (F) passes, else none. The identity \(\dot\sigma = c\,(a - a^*)\) is no force: it holds for any orbit, and below a 45° half-amplitude L4 and R0 cannot tell a force from it. A resonant or transient MMR row with F none is at most CONFIDENCE_GEOMETRY_ONLY_MAX (low): the angle is bounded, the mechanism is not shown (no resonant/transient by the geometry alone; Gallardo, Coito & Badano 2016, p. 89, show librating angles without a dynamical effect).
    status level
    resonant, transient V ambiguous or N < CONFIDENCE_MEDIUM_CYCLES (1.5: one closed cycle, a single 1:1 leg) → low; N ≥ CONFIDENCE_CERTAIN_CYCLES (3), at least CONFIDENCE_CERTAIN_CORE_CYCLES (3) observed cycles \((n_{TP}-1)/2\) inside the decisive episode, with M, S and V clean → certain; N ≥ CONFIDENCE_HIGH_CYCLES (2) with V clean or partial → high; otherwise medium; MMR with F none: at most low
    near_separatrix medium (low when V is ambiguous)
    non_resonant by a veto medium when the veto is thin or a second capture is unresolved; else high (a stable vetoed episode is still drawn as a candidate)
    non_resonant medium with a candidate (a leg, a half-swing, a near-episode run); certain for a clear circulation (MMR: C1 and C2 full, an alias C5 and C2 full; long-period: P full and no turning point at any cutoff); else high. A secular or ZLK record too short for one cycle that the policy decides as ending in circulation with an end travel below \(1 +\) CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND turns is at most medium (input end_travel; the travel is a range of \(\sigma_f\), the swing included, so 1.09 turns may still be part of a slow libration: 143662 s+g5−g8−s3)

    The counts are the same for every resonance type: in units of its own period two returns of a 4-Myr secular libration carry the same information as two of a 500-yr one, so a long period is no reason for a lower count; what a long-period row lacks is said by V (a gate, the mode phases), not by N. The literature on resonance identification from integrations sets no cycle count (Nesvorný & Morbidelli 1998; Gallardo 2014; Huaman et al. 2017; Christou & Asher 2011): the numbers are conventions. The level never falls as N rises. Caps become flags; confidence_raw equals confidence; the calibration cell is not reported. The action: extend by the extend rule, resample when the alias rule withdrew a needed check (the output step folds a short period, rsp_short_period_unmeasured), review for uncertain or for a needed veto beyond the extend budget, then medium spot_check, low review.

    The full tree: margins and caps¶

    The rest of this page is the confidence of the full status tree (its level, in v2_verdict); its margins stay columns.

    Margins¶

    Every test that can support or undermine the status is graded as a margin:

    margin meaning
    full the test passed with room to spare
    thin it passed, but close to its threshold, or it rests on too little (one cycle, one window)
    fail it did not pass
    not_measured it applies, but the record could not measure it; counts as thin (a check that could not be made is not a pass)
    not_applicable the test has no meaning for this verdict (e.g. the onset of a capture for a forced wave)

    Each margin belongs to a class:

    • D, decisive: a test the status tree itself relied on. In a final verdict it cannot fail (the verdict would be a different one); it can only be full, thin or unmeasured.
    • S, support: a test that may fail without changing the status; each failure lowers the level.
    • cap: a test whose failure limits the level directly (below).

    Margin U. Many thresholds are conventions rather than physics: chosen values such as \(R^2 \ge 0.7\). When a decision of the tree compared a value with a convention and the value lay within CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND = 10 % of it, \(\lvert x/x_{\rm thr} - 1\rvert < 0.1\), that decision would flip under a slightly different convention. It becomes a thin margin U:<name of the comparison>. Thresholds that follow from physics (one full turn, \(h = 1\) at the separatrix) never make a margin U. Which settings are conventions is marked "(convention)" in resonances/.env.dist.

    The margins of a mean-motion resonance¶

    id class physical question full when
    L1 D Does the libration survive a different filter? the decisive episode keeps ≥ MMR_L1_TP_FULL = 7 turning points at \(P_c/2\), \(P_c\) and \(2P_c\)
    L2 D Is it far from the separatrix? no cycle lies in the separatrix zone; without a measured zone, amplitude ≤ MMR_L2_AMP_FULL_DEG = 120° (thin to MMR_L2_AMP_THIN_DEG = 160°)
    L3 S Does \(\dot\sigma \simeq c\,(a - a_*)\) hold? the identity passes (not applicable to a kinematic verdict)
    L4 D Does \(\dot a\) follow the force law? \(R^2 \ge\) MMR_FORCE_R2_FULL = 0.7 in both halves of the record
    L5 S Is it one pendulum? \(r\) = max/min of \(T_0\) over the amplitude regimes ≤ \(b\) = MMR_L5_RATIO_FULL = 1.15 × \(\omega_{\max}/\omega_{\min}\) (the modulation of the force; 1 without one); thin to MMR_L5_RATIO_FAIL = 2 × \(b\), failed above; above \(b\) only thin when the median \(e\) > MMR_L5_E_CAVEAT = 0.2
    L6 D Is the center fixed? the center shifts by ≤ MMR_L6_SHIFT_FULL = 0.5 of the mean amplitude
    L7 D Does the libration reach both ends of the record? the gaps at the edges are ≤ MMR_L7_GAP_FULL = 0.7 periods
    L8 S Do the step and the filter resolve the cycle? \(\sigma\) turns by < MMR_L8_STEP_FULL_DEG = 10° per step, the filter keeps ≥ MMR_L8_GAIN_FULL = 0.94 of the cycle (failed below 0.5), no aliased line in the band; not graded for a circulation ≥ MMR_FAR_FROM_RES_FACTOR = 10 \(a\)-scales from \(a_*\)
    O1 cap Is this a capture, not a wave that was already there? the \(a\)-wave before the episode is < MMR_O1_RATIO_THIN = 0.25 of the one inside (≥ MMR_O1_RATIO_FAIL = 0.5 fails, cap low, only when that wave is coherent at \(P\), in phase and not the episode's own law; else thin)
    Z D Is the separatrix zone measured? measured without caveats
    R0 D Is the energy from \(a\) conserved in a potential of \(\sigma\)? passes
    S D Does a member of the multiplet own the libration? see below
    W D Does another resonance own it? see below
    C1–C5 D/S (circulation) Is it really a circulation? see below
    K1 D (kinematic) Does the angle follow the geometry? rate correlation ≥ MMR_K1_FULL = 0.97
    B5 S Is a libration slower than the record excluded? the center's rate is ≥ MMR_B5_RATIO_FULL = 2 × the slowest rate a libration in the record's range of \(a\) would allow
    F S Is the required strength available? required / theory within [1/x, x], x = MMR_STRENGTH_CHECK_RATIO = 10 (inverted for a kinematic verdict, below)
    N cap Was the record one turn short of an episode? no near-episode

    What each test measures is explained in Is it this resonance? and What the angle does.

    S and W support whichever origin the verdict names; they are not tests of absence only.

    • S, an accepted member: full when the member's theoretical check confirms it and no tie between members was broken by order alone; otherwise thin. No accepted member: the closest member's center range over \(\sigma\)'s, full above MMR_S_CLOSE_RATIO = 0.8 (a member nearly as still as \(\sigma\) is a near miss: thin).
    • W, an accepted owner: full when its median \(R^2 \ge\) MMR_W_R2_FULL = 0.85 and its force is reachable (for the potential route: every window scale within \([1/1.5,\ 1.5]\), MMR_OWNER_POTENTIAL_SCALE_FULL); an owner covering less than MMR_OWNER_COVER_MIN of the episode is thin. No owner: full while the best candidate's \(R^2\) stays below MMR_W_R2_NONE = 0.4. W is not measured when the phases cannot separate the best candidate or the search could not run, and not applicable to 1:1.

    C1–C5 grade a circulation:

    • C1: the center passes \(\ge\) MMR_C1_REV_FULL = 10 turns (not for an alias);
    • C2: full when no turning point is found at \(P_c\), \(P_c/2\) or \(2P_c\); otherwise thin;
    • C3: full when the rate never runs against its mean direction, its modulation is below MMR_C3_MODULATION_MAX = 0.3 and pattern B is absent; otherwise thin. The modulation is \(\sqrt{b_c^2 + b_s^2}/\lvert b_0\rvert\) from the fit \(\dot\sigma = b_0 + b_c\cos\sigma + b_s\sin\sigma\);
    • C4: \(r = \langle\lvert\dot\sigma\rvert\rangle / (2\sqrt{\lvert c\rvert K})\), \(K\) the fitted force amplitude: the rate against the fastest a pendulum of that force could turn at the separatrix. Full for \(r \ge\) MMR_C4_RATIO_FULL = 1.5, thin for \(1 < r < 1.5\), failed for \(r \le 1\). A failure counts as not measured when no force law explains \(\dot a\) anywhere (best \(R^2 <\) MMR_FORCE_R2_FAIL = 0.4). Not for an alias;
    • C5, resolved step: full when the measured rate and the rate expected from the mean motions have the same sign and agree within MMR_C5_RATE_TOL = 0.2; otherwise thin. On an alias: full only when the far ratio reaches MMR_FAR_FROM_RES_FACTOR = 10 and the distance from \(\nu\Delta t\) to the nearest half-turn exceeds MMR_C5_BRANCH_RESID_FACTOR = 2 × the largest unwrap residual of a step; otherwise thin.

    F for a kinematic verdict. For a verdict that claims this resonance's own dynamics, F grades the agreement of required and theory. For a non_resonant verdict of kinematic origin with a directly computed strength (direct averaging or the extended-precision harmonic), F reads in the inverted sense: full when the strength check fails (the force available cannot make the observed cycle, which supports the kinematic reading), not measured otherwise. The order-of-magnitude \(e^q\) scale gives no such margin. The column check_mmr_strength keeps its ordinary meaning.

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai margins¶

    N (turning points of the decisive episode; thin at the minimum of three), S (the center stationary over enough independent cycles), F (the libration period ≥ FILTER_MIN_LIBRATION_CUTOFFS = 2 × \(P_c\)), P (a circulation's center passes a full turn at every smoothing) and, for Lidov-Kozai, G (the free-\(\omega\) gate decided with its number of cycles clear of FREE_GATE_MIN_CYCLES = 3 by more than the thin band).

    From margins to a level¶

    The raw level, confidence_raw:

    level rule
    certain every applicable margin full: no thin, no unmeasured and no U margin
    high no failure; any number of thin (or unmeasured) margins
    medium exactly one failed support margin
    low two or more failed support margins

    The shown level, confidence, is the raw level lowered by every cap the verdict carries: \(\text{confidence} = \min(\text{raw},\ \text{ceilings of the caps})\). A failed cap margin (O1, N) is not counted as a failure: its cap does the limiting, so the figure lists the limits apart from the support margins.

    A catalog row that takes the status of its accepted member (subresonance_librates) takes the member's confidence too, with its raw level, thin and failed margins and caps; its mrg_* columns and calibration cell stay its own. Read the member's row (owner) to see why.

    One cap is general: when none of the physics of the angle's own force could be measured (for an MMR: the force law L4, the potential R0, the zone Z, the owner search W, and the strength F by direct averaging), the level is at most medium (all_physics_not_measured). The identity and the response of \(a\) (L3, B1, L5) are not in this list: they show that \(a\) carries the oscillation, which is just as true of a libration induced by another resonance. A kinematic verdict has one more physics margin, K1e: the episode's \(\sigma\) follows \(p\varpi\), \(a\) is flat (B1 < MMR_KIN_B1_CLEAR) and the period is a secular one, all three together (kinematic origins); when it is full the cap does not apply and the kinematic libration can reach high (rule kinematic_high).

    A verdict that claims the angle's own libration (resonant, transient or near_separatrix of its own dynamics) is high only when at least one physical support of the own force passes with its full margin: the force law L4 (for a 1:1 angle the co-orbital law, which takes L4), the potential R0 or the strength F by direct averaging. Otherwise the level is at most medium (no_full_physics_support, MMR_HIGH_NEEDS_FULL_SUPPORT). The zone Z and the owner search W exclude rivals and do not count as a support; when every physics margin is not_measured, all_physics_not_measured applies instead. Kinematic, induced and subresonance verdicts rest on other evidence and are not limited by it.

    Caps and the other reading¶

    A cap names a specific way the record could be read differently. Every cap that brings the level down to medium or low names, in the right column of the combined figure, the verdict the record might hold instead and the panel that decides between the two.

    cap ceiling the other reading
    three_tp_episode high no libration yet: three turning points are one cycle
    zone_not_measured high the cycles may sit in the separatrix zone
    exit_suspected, entry_suspected medium the libration goes on past the record's edge, or \(\sigma\) leaves (enters) only here
    one_return_cycle medium no libration yet: one cycle closed only by its phase return (two turning points)
    transient_unexplained medium near_separatrix: \(\sigma\) wanders across the separatrix without a capture or an escape
    episodes_in_separatrix_zone medium the edges of the librating stretches are crossings of the zone, not a capture or an escape
    force_law_disagrees medium uncertain: the potential holds while \(\dot a\) does not follow \(\sin\sigma\)
    episode_force_law_mixed medium only one episode librates in this resonance; the others carry another force
    episode_before_jump medium the episodes belong to a regime that ended with the jump of \(a\)
    action_step_provisional medium one libration over the whole window: the step in amplitude is a large modulation, not two energy levels
    slow_libration_not_excluded medium a slower libration of \(\sigma\) about a moving center
    near_episode medium transient: one more turning point would make a librating episode (a 1:1 leg between passages, or one whose two turns are kicks, counts: leaf G4c)
    near_separatrix_without_zone, l2_without_zone medium a circulation, or a libration close to the separatrix
    second_capture_unresolved medium \(\sigma\) is held by its own term as well: both resonances at once
    owner_search_skipped, catalogue_longitudes medium another resonance drives \(a\) (rerun with the search on, the planets saved)
    planets_missing, nodal_subresonance_candidate, subresonance_tie, subresonance_t6_only, subresonance_theory_unconfirmed, subresonance_undetermined medium a member of the multiplet holds the center
    jitter_low_e medium invalid: \(\varpi\) is undefined at small \(e\)
    short_period_aliased medium the turning points are partly the output step's: short-period terms folded into \(\sigma_f\) and \(a_f\)
    one_cycle_energy_not_conserved medium (1:1) the one cycle may be a passage, not motion in the averaged well
    record_ends_unresolved medium a capture at the end of the record: after circulating, \(\sigma_f\) slowed to an arc or a stall that lasts longer than twice its longest revolution (the record's edges); a longer integration forward decides
    one_coorbital_leg medium (1:1) uncertain: the transient rests on one clean leg between passages (law, energy, two reflecting turns), no closed cycle
    strength_scale_only medium uncertain: only the order-of-magnitude strength agrees, the force is not computed
    all_physics_not_measured medium uncertain: nothing measured shows this angle's own force
    no_full_physics_support medium the oscillation is not this angle's own: each physical support is only thin
    force_law_theory_only medium uncertain: the slow \(\dot a\) fits the averaged force of the term, but the harmonic force law fails; a rival force hidden by the smoothing is not excluded
    slow_scale_provisional medium L4 untested: read at a shorter cutoff of the scale ladder (signal) with neither the force law nor R0 with \(a\) measured; a record of \(\ge 5.25\,P\) decides
    geometry_only low uncertain: only the shape of \(\sigma\) says libration
    implausible_strength low a libration whose force the first-order average misses
    onset_failed low non_resonant: \(\sigma\) rides a coherent \(a\)-wave at its period that began before the episode, continues into it in phase and is not this resonance's own force

    Calibration¶

    How often a level was right is reported next to it, never mixed into it. The verdicts are grouped into cells (status, angle_status, reason). For each cell, the benchmark (Benchmark) counts the author-labeled verdicts at high or certain, \(n\), and the wrong ones among them, \(n_{\rm err}\). With \(\hat p = (n - n_{\rm err})/n\) and \(z = 1.96\), the Wilson (1927) 95 % interval of the accuracy is

    \[ \frac{\hat p + \dfrac{z^2}{2n} \pm z\sqrt{\dfrac{\hat p(1-\hat p)}{n} + \dfrac{z^2}{4n^2}}}{1 + \dfrac{z^2}{n}} . \]

    The columns cell_n_examples, cell_n_errors, cell_accuracy_low_95 and cell_accuracy_high_95 carry them (from resonances/data/confidence_cells.json, written by benchmark/tools/calibrate.py). With tens of labels per cell the interval is wide. The level is therefore ordinal evidence: certain is stronger evidence than high, which is stronger than medium; it is not a probability that the status is right.

    Actions¶

    Each verdict ends with an action, the cheapest step that would make it stronger:

    action when
    none certain (always, even when a longer record could still test something) or high
    spot_check medium: look at the figure
    review low; uncertain / origin_unknown; invalid other than an alias or an undersampled step; a record whose period already fits it while it is still too short to decide (more time cures nothing)
    extend other uncertain verdicts, and an uncertain / origin_unknown one whose deciding measurement is missing only for lack of length (below): integrate longer, to extend_to_years
    resample or review an uncertain 1:1 stall that stays long enough in the quasi-satellite well (G1q) with \(P_{QS} < P_c\): the filter erased a possible quasi-satellite libration, so more time at the same step cures nothing. resample when the record's step cannot hold the quasi-satellite cutoff, else review
    resample invalid / alias or undersampled, or a level held down by a check the folded short periods withdrew (R-ALIAS-A, rsp_short_period_unmeasured): integrate again with the output step resample_step_yr

    How long to extend. A libration needs at least \(N_{\rm TP}/2\) periods plus the edges the filter trims:

    \[ T_{\rm req} = \frac{N_{\rm TP}}{2}\,P + 2\,f_{\rm trim}\,P_c , \]

    with \(N_{\rm TP}\) = LIBRATION_MIN_TURNING_POINTS = 3, \(f_{\rm trim}\) = FILTER_EDGE_TRIM_CUTOFFS = 1 and \(P\) the time scale that decides: the measured cycle, or for a too-short record the slowest lower bound its shape allows (twice the spacing of its turning points, a slow arc of the center, the rate of a stall). The physical estimate is \(N\), a round value \(\ge \max(T_{\rm req},\ f_{\min} T)\), \(f_{\min}\) = MMR_EXTEND_MIN_SPAN_FACTOR = 2, \(T\) the record length (column base_extend_to_years). For insufficient_time the action asks only for the next check,

    \[ T_{\rm next} = \mathrm{round\ up}\big(\max(f_{\min}T,\ \min(N,\ f_{\rm next}T))\big), \]

    with \(f_{\rm next}\) = MMR_NEXT_CHECK_SPAN_FACTOR = 4: a budget after which the record is analyzed again, not a promise that it is enough. A drift or another uncertain verdict asks for \(N\) itself. When \(T_{\rm req} \le T\), time is not what is missing: base_cause names what is, and the action is review.

    Length-limited origin_unknown (rule extend_length_limited). Leaves 5e, 5g and 5x are decided while the force law L4 is not measured; leaf 5n by one independent cycle. When L4 has fewer than MMR_FORCE_MIN_WINDOWS = 2 windows of MMR_FORCE_WINDOW_CYCLES = 3.5 periods (not when the swing is below 45° with enough windows, nor for a forced line), or 5n's decisive episode is the last one, and the last librating episode reaches the record's end, the action is extend to

    \[ T_{\rm FW} = \mathrm{round\ up}\big(t_{\rm start} + n_{\rm FW}\, W\, P\big) \]

    for the windows (\(t_{\rm start}\), \(P\) of that last episode, \(n_{\rm FW}\) = MMR_EXTEND_FORCE_WINDOWS = 2, \(W\) = MMR_FORCE_WINDOW_CYCLES; 2003 TX9 3E+2J-10: \(2210 + 2 \cdot 3.5 \cdot 3136\) → 30 kyr), and to \(T_{\rm next}\) with \(N\) = the last turning point + the missing turning points × \(P/2\) + \(f_{\rm trim} P_c\) for 5n (2003 ND 6J-6S-1: 40 kyr; its L4 windows ask for 60 kyr), whichever is longer. An episode that ended before the record's end gains nothing from a longer record: review. A failed check (5a, 5b, 5o, R0 of 5e) stays: the extension serves the regime at the end (TX9's early R0 failure remains). No status changes.

    The flag extend_time (rule end_arc_extend) is not an action: it says that a longer integration in extend_direction (forward, backward, both) would show how a record edge ends, whatever the action (the record's edges). Without an estimate its length is the next check \(T_{\rm next}\) with \(N\) unknown, round up(\(f_{\min} T\)) (2022 BB2: 1 kyr → 2 kyr); with action extend the action's length stays in extend_to_years. A backward extension means integrating from the record's start into the past (2008 EZ84's one reflection at 144 yr). No status or level changes; a review row stays at review.

    Which time scale decides (base_period_source)

    For few_cycles: twice the median spacing of the turning points, and the slow scales. For arc: twice the longest stretch without a turn of the slow center, the \(2\omega\) or \(e\)-cycle when the kinematic test fails, and B5's bound. For stall: one revolution at the stall's mean rate, and the force bound \(P_\text{lib,min}\) (The lower bound of an arc or a stall). The column base_period_source names the one that decided.

    Which step to resample at. For an alias or a coarse step, resample_step_yr is the largest value of MMR_RESAMPLE_STEPS_YR that keeps one alias branch over the range of \(a\), samples the libration at least MMR_RESAMPLE_STEPS_PER_CYCLE = 20 times per cycle and lets the filter exist (\(P_c > 2\Delta t\)); review when the record's step already meets these bounds. When a check the folded short periods withdrew holds the level down, the step is smp_fold_free_step_yr: the largest grid step below the record's at which no short period folds.

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai. The same table, and every extension is a next check \(T_{\rm next}\) with an \(N\) by cause (column base_extend_cause; module analysis/secular/extend.py). No status changes:

    cause \(N\) action
    baseline (baseline_needed: drift, insufficient_time) \(T_{\rm req}\) with \(P = \max(P_{\rm floor}, P_{\rm cyc})\), \(P_c\) the decisive cutoff (secular with no cycle, \(P_c\) = 100 kyr: 500 kyr) extend
    gate_cycles (gate short_window) \(n_{\min} P_{\rm cyc}\) + what the free elements lose of the record (\(T - T_{\rm free}\)), \(n_{\min}\) = FREE_GATE_MIN_CYCLES extend
    gate_resolution (gate planetary_residual) a free rate \(d_{\min}\)″/yr from a planetary one, inside the Rayleigh width \(1\,296\,000/T_{\rm free}\): \(m \cdot 1\,296\,000/d_{\min} + (T - T_{\rm free})\), \(m\) = ZLK_EXTEND_RESOLUTION_MARGIN = 1.25 (373136 at 100 kyr, \(d\) = 3.5: 400 kyr; 1981 at 500 kyr, \(d\) = 1.9: 1 Myr) extend
    gate_unresolvable that baseline above ZLK_EXTEND_MAX_YR = 100 Myr, or \(d_{\min} = 0\) review
    gate_undefined_phase, gate_unavailable, unusable_series a longer record does not define the free phase, build missing free elements or repair a series review

    The geometric \(T_{\rm req}\) joins every gate cause (the larger \(N\)). Columns as for an MMR: base_required_kyr, base_period_yr, base_period_source (cycle or period_floor), base_extend_to_years.
