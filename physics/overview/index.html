    How the status is determined¶

    The package integrates the orbit of a small body together with the planets, builds the resonant angle \(\sigma(t)\) of every resonance it checks, and gives each angle a verdict. This section explains, in physical terms, how that verdict is reached. Each test is stated as the physical question it answers, then as a formula, then as the threshold that decides it, with the name of the setting that holds the threshold (Configuration).

    The verdict answers two separate questions:

    1. What does the angle do? Does \(\sigma\) librate (oscillate about a center), circulate, drift, or do both in turn? This is pure geometry of the filtered angle (What the angle does).
    2. Is the body in this resonance? A libration of \(\sigma\) alone is not enough. For a mean-motion resonance the test is whether the resonant term drives the oscillation of the semi-major axis \(a\): \(a\) must oscillate with \(\sigma\) in a definite way (The resonant pendulum, Is it this resonance?). Secular and Lidov-Kozai angles have their own tests (Secular and Lidov-Kozai). An angle can also look bounded because of the perihelion precession, another resonance nearby, or a too coarse output step.

    What a verdict claims

    A verdict describes the saved interval of the integration, at the saved output step, for the model of the run (the planets that were integrated, the integrator, the initial elements). A longer integration, a finer step or another epoch can give a different answer. The thresholds are conventions, chosen and tested on a benchmark of examples labeled by eye (Benchmark); all of them are settings.

    Distant bodies: records of 300 kyr or more

    Beyond a planet's 1:2 (trans-Neptunian objects, outer Centaurs) the cutoff is at least 1.5 times the body's own orbital period, several hundred years (The cutoff), and the librations are slow. Integrate such bodies for at least 300 kyr so that several cycles fit in the record; the classification of the outer Solar System uses 10 Myr (Gladman, Marsden & VanLaerhoven 2008).

    The verdict¶

    field values meaning
    status resonant \(\sigma\) librates over the whole record. Whether its dynamical origin was established is in reason and libration_origin: dynamic_libration/dynamic is a measured explanation, geometric_libration with origin unknown a geometric classification whose force was not measured
    transient \(\sigma\) librates in one or more episodes that do not cover the record as one libration (a capture, an escape, a drift or another regime separates them)
    near_separatrix no full libration cycle, but \(\sigma\) repeatedly stalls or slips at the top of its own potential well while \(a\) responds
    non_resonant \(\sigma\) circulates, or it is bounded by something other than this resonance
    uncertain the record cannot decide: too short, an unexplained drift, or contradicting physics
    invalid the record cannot be measured at these settings: an aliased or too coarse output step, an unphysical orbit
    angle_status libration, episodic, near_separatrix, circulation, drift, insufficient_time, undefined the geometry of \(\sigma\) over the whole record (What the angle does)
    angle_subtype stationary, modulated, moving_centre, regime_change; recurrent; pattern_a, pattern_b; alias; arc, stall, few_cycles; unphysical, undefined_phase the kind of angle_status
    final_regime, final_regime_start_yr libration, circulation, drift, near_separatrix, insufficient_time, empty how the record ends (a capture, an exit) and from when
    orbit_type tadpole L4, horseshoe, interior, symmetric libration about 0°, … the header of the figure; descriptive, decides nothing
    reason e.g. dynamic_libration, kinematic_libration, alias the one main explanation of the status (every reason)
    libration_origin dynamic, induced, subresonance, kinematic, joint, unknown who makes the bounded oscillation (joint: both resonances at once, measured)
    origin_detail varpi_term, secular_cycle, planet_ripple, owner_libration, owner_forced_wave, fixed_subresonance, composite_multiplet, free_omega_winds, joint_capture, forced_wave_background (every counted episode is the forced wave its background carries and the slow center passes a revolution: non-resonant, veto forced_wave) the concrete cause (Is it this resonance?)
    owner e.g. 11M-20+0+9 the resonance that carries the oscillation (induced, subresonance), or the partner of a joint capture
    confidence, confidence_raw certain, high, medium, low how well the evidence supports the status, after and before the caps (Confidence); empty for uncertain and invalid
    action none, spot_check, review, extend, resample what to do next with this body (Actions)
    check_mmr_* pass, fail, not_measured, not_applicable each physical test: pass supports the resonant reading, fail refutes it
    flags, caps, thin_margins, failed_support ;-separated lists what lowered the confidence and why

    A body can therefore be non_resonant while angle_status = libration: the angle librates, but not because of this resonance. The origins of such a kinematic or induced libration are explained in Is it this resonance?. Every column of the output files is listed in Output data.

    Limitation: the ends of the record

    The classifier reads the record without its ends: about one filter cutoff (\(P_c\) × FILTER_EDGE_TRIM_CUTOFFS) at each end, where the filter spreads. An episode under way at the start or at the end of the record whose turning points fall in that trimmed stretch is not counted, so the status describes the record, not the state at the epoch (2022 YG: its present quasi-satellite, about 16–134 yr, is mostly inside the 55-yr trim at the start). To classify the state at a date, put that date at least one cutoff inside the record (integrate backward and forward). For a 1:1 angle the coverage audit counts such stretches in aud_edge_half_cycles (Output).

    The pipeline¶

    flowchart LR
    A[integration<br/>saved every Δt] --> B[signal<br/>σ, a, cutoff, filter]
    B --> C[geometry<br/>turning points, cycles,<br/>episodes, center]
    C --> D[physics<br/>identity, force law,<br/>energy, strength, owner]
    D --> E[status tree]
    E --> F[confidence<br/>and action]
    1. Signal. Is the output step fine enough? The angle is unwrapped and low-pass filtered so that the short-period terms (orbital and synodic periods) are removed and the libration is kept.
    2. Geometry. Turning points of the filtered angle, closed cycles, librating episodes, the center of each episode, jumps of \(a\). This gives angle_status.
    3. Physics. Does \(a\) follow \(\sigma\) as the pendulum of this resonance requires? Is the strength needed for the observed period available? Does another resonance own the oscillation?
    4. Status tree. The geometry and the physics are combined, in a fixed order, into status, reason and libration_origin.
    5. Confidence. Every comparison of the analysis has a margin; the margins and a few caps give the confidence level and the suggested action.
    The exact order of the steps (MMR)

    analyse_mmr (resonances/resonance/analysis/mmr/analyse.py) runs in a fixed order: input check → context (rates, \(a_*\), \(c\), synodic periods, the great inequality) → sampling (predictive unwrap, alias) → preprocessing (ripple, cutoff, filter) → geometry → slow parts → response of \(a\) → energy and zone → kinematics → angle_status → filter guard → subresonances → strength → owner (with that strength) → the verdicts of derived members → status → margins, caps, confidence.

    The analysis reads only the saved series, so a run can be classified again without integrating (SimulationSerializer.restore(..., recompute_librations=True), benchmark/tools/reclassify.py). Every comparison is written to a ledger (decision_path, the right-hand column of the combined figure), and every threshold is a setting. The rule IDs used in the figure and the output columns (G0, L4, 5u, …) are listed in the Rule index.

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai angles go through the same geometry with their own signal step and physics (Secular and Lidov-Kozai). The Gallery shows one benchmark example of every status with its one-page figure.

    Notation¶

    symbol meaning
    \(\lambda, \varpi, \Omega\) mean longitude, longitude of perihelion, longitude of the node of the body
    \(\lambda_j, \varpi_j, \Omega_j\) the same for planet \(j\)
    \(k, k_j\) integer coefficients of \(\lambda\) and \(\lambda_j\) in the resonant angle
    \(q\) the order of an MMR: \(q = \lvert k + \sum_j k_j\rvert\) (prograde two- and three-body); \(p' + q'\) for a retrograde \(p'/{-q'}\) resonance
    \(n, n_j\) mean motions; \(n(a) = 2\pi\,a^{-3/2}\) rad/yr with \(a\) in au
    \(\nu = \dot\sigma\) the rate of the angle; \(a_*\) the exact resonance, \(\nu(a_*) = 0\)
    \(c = \partial\nu/\partial a\) how fast the angle's rate changes with \(a\); \(c = -3kn/(2a)\)
    \(\sigma_c(t)\), \(\psi = \sigma - \sigma_c\) the center of libration and the angle measured from it
    \(\omega_0\) the small-amplitude libration frequency; \(T_0 = 2\pi/\omega_0\)
    \(A\) the libration amplitude (half the peak-to-peak swing of \(\sigma\))
    \(P\), \(P_i\) the libration period, the period of cycle \(i\)
    \(\Delta t\) the output step of the integration
    \(P_c\) the cutoff period of the low-pass filter
    \(\mu\), \(\mu_j\) planet-to-Sun mass ratios
    \(\mathsf K(m)\), \(\mathsf E(m)\) complete elliptic integrals of the first and second kind, with parameter \(m\)

    Time is in years, angles in radians (degrees in the output columns whose names end in _deg), \(a\) in au.
