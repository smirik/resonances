    Secular and Lidov-Kozai angles¶

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai (ZLK) angles evolve over \(10^4\)–\(10^7\) yr instead of \(10^2\)–\(10^4\) yr, and they involve only the slow angles \(\varpi\), \(\Omega\), \(\omega\). They go through the same geometry as mean-motion resonances (turning points, cycles, episodes, coverage), with their own signal step and their own physics. The status tree is short (below): there is no semi-major axis to respond, no owner and no subresonance.

    analysis.secular.analyse_secular(series, settings) and analysis.zlk.analyse_zlk(series, settings, free_elements=...) read a LongPeriodSeries: the classified angle (the reduced proper angle of a secular resonance, \(\omega\) for ZLK) with \(a\), \(e\), \(i\), \(\varpi\), \(\Omega\) on a uniform grid of years. Simulation.identify_librations calls them on every path (a run, a batch worker, restore(recompute_librations=True)).

    The steps of the analysis
    step what
    0 input an unphysical orbit (\(e > 1.3\), \(a < 0\), non-finite \(a\) or \(e\)) → invalid / unphysical; fewer than three samples, or non-finite times or angles → uncertain / unusable_series (angle undefined in both)
    1 cutoff \(P_c\) and the turning-point prominence (below)
    2 filter \(\sigma_f\) at \(P_c\), \(P_c/2\) and \(2P_c\); the window \([t_0 + P_c,\ t_1 - P_c]\). When \(\sigma\) cannot be filtered at \(P_c\) (a grid not uniform and increasing within FILTER_GRID_RTOL, or \(P_c \le 2\Delta t\)) → uncertain / unusable_series, no geometry (an MMR gives invalid / undersampled there)
    3 geometry turning points, runs, episodes, the center and its passes, exit and entry (shared with MMRs)
    4 angle angle_status from the geometry (below); without a libration at \(P_c\), the same at the scale of the center
    5 physics secular: the \(e\)/\(i\) response (a column); ZLK: the free-\(\omega\) gate
    6 status analysis.secular.decide.decide_status
    7 confidence the margins N, S, F, P, G and the caps (below)

    The secular angle¶

    What should librate? A linear secular resonance locks a precession frequency of the body to one of the planetary system's eigenfrequencies: \(g \approx g_6\) (\(\nu_6\)), \(s \approx s_6\) (\(\nu_{16}\)), or a combination such as \(g + s - g_6 - s_6\) (\(z_1\)). In the Laplace-Lagrange theory the planets' eccentricity and inclination vectors are sums of eigenmodes, each turning at its own rate \(g_j\) or \(s_j\) (Murray & Dermott 1999, eqs. 7.28–7.29, p. 278). The resonant angle therefore compares the body's \(\varpi\) and \(\Omega\) with the planets' mode longitudes, not with their osculating elements, after removing the forced terms that are not in the angle. Why both matter, and the measured phases: Secular resonances.

    The angle σ and the reduced vectors

    The mode longitudes are

    \[ \ell_{g_j}(t) = g_j\,(t + t_{\rm epoch}) + \phi_{g_j}, \qquad \ell_{s_j}(t) = s_j\,(t + t_{\rm epoch}) + \phi_{s_j}, \]

    with the phases quoted at J2000 (\(t_{\rm epoch}\) the offset of the run's start from J2000).

    Units. In the formulas of this page \(g\), \(s\) and every frequency \(f\) are angular frequencies in rad/yr. The stored PLANETARY_FREQUENCIES are in ″/yr and are multiplied by \(\pi/648\,000\); one cycle per year is 1 296 000 ″/yr. A record of length \(T\) resolves frequencies \(2\pi/T\) rad/yr apart (one Rayleigh width, \(1/T\) in cycles/yr).

    The body enters through its complex eccentricity and inclination vectors, built from the saved (ecliptic) elements, \(z_e = e\,e^{i\varpi}\) and \(z_i = \sin\tfrac{i}{2}\,e^{i\Omega}\) (Murray & Dermott use \(I\) in radians; since \(\sin\frac i2 \simeq I/2\), phases and frequencies agree, while inclination amplitudes here are half theirs). The forced terms at the fundamentals that are not in the angle only shake it, so they are removed by a least-squares fit at their known frequencies \(f_j\):

    \[ z_{\rm red} = z - \sum_{j \in B} C_j\, e^{i f_j t}. \]

    The basis \(B\) is that of the free elements: \(g_5\)–\(g_8\) plus a constant for \(z_e\), and the constant (\(s_5 = 0\), the invariable plane) plus \(s_6\)–\(s_8\) for \(z_i\); inner-planet modes are not subtracted. Modes closer than two Rayleigh widths cannot be separated, so the candidates are first clustered and one representative of each cluster is kept; the representatives whose labels occur in the angle are dropped (their terms are the resonance), and the rest is fitted and removed. With \(\varpi = \arg z_{e,\rm red}\) and \(\Omega = \arg z_{i,\rm red}\):

    \[ \sigma = p\,\varpi + q\,\Omega + \sum_j \big(p_j\,\ell_{g_j} + q_j\,\ell_{s_j}\big), \qquad p + q + \sum_j (p_j + q_j) = 0 . \]

    Signal: what is not the libration¶

    The filter must remove everything faster than the shortest-period libration allowed, and nothing slower:

    \[ P_c = \max\Big(F_{\rm GI}\,P_{\rm GI},\ \ F_w \max_j \frac{2\pi}{\lvert f - f_j\rvert},\ \ \frac{P_{\min}}{f_{\min}}\Big). \]
    • \(P_{\rm GI}\) = 937 yr, the Jupiter-Saturn great inequality, the longest short-period term of an asteroid's elements; \(F_{\rm GI}\) = SECULAR_FILTER_GI_FACTOR = 2.
    • For an angle with two or more planetary modes, each mode \(j\) makes a forced wave: its forced term turns relative to the free one at \(f - f_j\) (\(f\) the body's own \(g\) or \(s\)). These waves are not librations; \(F_w\) = SECULAR_FILTER_FORCED_FACTOR = 2. A single-mode angle (\(\nu_6\)) has none: its forced term is the resonance.
    • \(P_{\min}\) = SECULAR_LIBRATION_PERIOD_MIN_YR = 200 kyr, the shortest secular libration accepted (a convention: the widest linear resonances are a few ″/yr wide, so their periods exceed ~1 Myr); \(f_{\min}\) = FILTER_MIN_LIBRATION_CUTOFFS = 2.

    What periods to expect, and why the filter is not stronger. A local resonant Hamiltonian \(H = H_0(I) + A(I)\cos\sigma\) gives the small-amplitude period \(P_0 = 2\pi/\sqrt{\lvert KA\rvert}\), with \(K\) the torsion \(\partial\delta/\partial I\) of the divisor along the angle's canonical action and \(A\) the resonant coefficient (Morbidelli 2002, pp. 72–74; Morbidelli & Henrard 1991, Eqs. 35–39); the period grows towards the separatrix. Neither \(K\) nor \(A\) follows from one body's proper elements and frequencies, and the package measures neither, so no period is predicted per body. Published cycles span sub-Myr to ~8 Myr (\(z_1\): (339) Dorothea 4.15 Myr, (221) Eos 3.4 Myr, Milani & Knežević 1992, p. 227; (3701) Purkyne ~8 Myr, Vokrouhlický et al. 2006; \(z_2\): (8089) Yukar ~6.1 Myr, Carruba et al. 2016). The \(\nu_6\) librators of the benchmark (0.24–0.57 Myr) repeat at the free/forced beat \(2\pi/\lvert g_{\rm free} - g_6\rvert\) within 1 %: a forced oscillator, not a pendulum. No general lower bound on the period exists, so a filter set above a real libration's period would erase it (\(G(P) = 1/[1 + (P_c/P)^4]\) keeps half at \(P = P_c\)): the slower scales are tried only where the record shows a slow motion (the center scale below).

    The output step does not enter. The filter acts on the unwrapped angle with its trend removed and restored, so it cannot turn a circulation into a libration. A turning point must stand out by at least \(\max(5°,\ 4r)\), where \(r\) is the 95th percentile of what the filtered angle still holds faster than \(P_{\rm floor} = \max(P_{\min},\ 2P_c)\), the same shortest period as for an episode (SECULAR_TP_PROMINENCE_DEG, SECULAR_TP_RIPPLE_FACTOR; \(r = 0\) when \(P_{\rm floor} \le P_c\)). The two filters split softly, so a libration slower than \(P_{\rm floor}\) leaves \(G(P; P_c) - G(P; P_{\rm floor})\) of its own swing in that difference; its image through the same filters is taken out first (as for an MMR, Geometry: turning points; geometry.libration_image), or a libration close to the floor raises its own threshold (591986 over 4 Myr: \(r\) = 4.35° and 17.4° against a 16° libration, cut into three episodes where its center moves fastest; 1.17° and 5° without the image, one episode).

    That \(r\) is the record's, and a circulation elsewhere in the record inflates it. So, as for an MMR, each run of turning points found at the floor (5°) is selected at the prominence measured on its own samples, its ends trimmed by \(P_{\rm floor}\) and the rest at least \(P_{\rm floor}\) long, and never above the record's (SECULAR_TP_LOCAL_PROMINENCE, ZLK_TP_LOCAL_PROMINENCE). 19289 s−s6 over 20 Myr: 76.8° from the record (26.8° outside the libration, 8.0° inside), 31.7° on the run: the libration from 5.1 Myr to the end is one episode instead of seven pieces. The cap: where faster waves hide the libration's period from the turning points at the floor, its image is not taken out and its own swing would count as fast on the run (2020 PP1, ZLK: 101° against 52°). A lower threshold may add libration but never remove it: every librating episode found at the record's prominence must lie inside one librating episode of the local reading, else the runs it overlaps keep the record's prominence. A run of provisional turning points can span a libration and the slow approach to it, and the approach's wiggles then become turning points too: 455327 g−s−g5+s6 (ias15, 20 Myr), 39.0° from the record and 13.8° on the run, a 32° wiggle at 9.1–9.5 Myr would start the run on the drifting approach (centre_moves) and lose the crescent episode 10.2–12.5 Myr.

    Geometry and status¶

    A librating episode needs LIBRATION_MIN_TURNING_POINTS = 3 turning points (max–min–max: one full cycle), a period not shorter than \(\max(P_{\min},\ 2P_c)\) (a faster one is a forced wave), and a center that does not run a full turn inside it. One that reaches both ends of the record, within LIBRATION_COVERAGE_PERIODS = 1 period, makes libration; otherwise the record is episodic.

    A bounded run whose center turns back. The center tests are those of Geometry (the bands of the cycles share a point, or the center turns back LIBRATION_CENTRE_RETURNS_MODULATED = 3 times), but a secular libration makes only a few cycles in a record, too few for three returns. So a run of a secular or ZLK angle that fails only for its moving center is accepted as modulated (center stationarity not measured) when it closes a cycle, its turning points span less than a turn, its cycle center turns back at least once within less than \(\pi\), and one of its cycles swings over every center it has (SECULAR_TURNING_CENTRE, ZLK_TURNING_CENTRE; geometry.accept_turning_centre). A center that moves one way stays a drift (101703 g−2g6+g7, a run at 13.4–19 Myr: −231°, no return), and so does a small wave carried by a slower motion: no cycle of it covers the track (363 g−g6+s−s6: half-amplitudes 4–6° on a center that travels 42°). 42946 g−g6+s−s6 over 20 Myr: the run 1.3–14.6 Myr (17 turning points, \(P\) = 1.6 Myr, centers 116–228° turning back twice, bands missing by 18°, the cycle about 183° of half-amplitude 93° covering them all) is the early libration episode.

    A ZLK island is half a turn wide. The averaged Lidov-Kozai Hamiltonian depends on \(2\omega\), so its islands (\(\omega\) about 90° and 270°) are at most half a turn wide. A run of \(\omega\) therefore breaks where a half-cycle swings ZLK_RUN_BREAK_HALF_AMPLITUDE_DEG = 90° or more, or its midpoint jumps by more (a secular or MMR angle: 180°). This only splits runs; which island an episode is in decides nothing. 88899 over 500 kyr: two islands (67–169 kyr about 90°, 191–294 kyr about 270°) that one run at 180° joined into a circulation (rule zlk_half_turn_cut).

    Drift needs a monotone center. A drift is a slow center that keeps going one way. When the runs are rejected for their moving center but the slow center turns back inside the record (away from the edges of its own low-pass) and its largest pass stays below a turn, the record holds part of one slow swing: insufficient_time / few_cycles, not drift (2306 \(g-g_6+s-s_6\): a turn at 2.04 Myr, largest pass 0.92 rev in 10 Myr; rule centre_turned_insufficient). Status and extension are the same as for a drift.

    A swing must exceed the uncertainty of the mode phases. Under SECULAR_PLANET_PHASES=linear the angle uses \(\ell_j = g_j t + \phi_j\) with constant phases, while the true phase \(\phi_j(t)\) of an inner mode wanders (Laskar 1990). Over a record of length \(T\) the angle then carries an error whose swing is at most

    \[ \Delta\phi(T) = \sum_j \lvert k_j\rvert\, \mathrm{range}_j(T), \]

    \(k_j\) the angle's coefficient of mode \(j\), \(\mathrm{range}_j(T)\) the largest max − min of \(\phi_j\) over a window of length \(T\), measured on the package's own model (resonances/data/mode_phase_uncertainty.json, at 10 Myr: \(g_4\) 30°, \(g_3\) 24°, \(s_4\) 81°, \(s_2\) 87°, \(g_2\) 2.7°, \(g_6\) 0.9°, \(s_6\) 9.5°; how it is measured). A libration or episode whose swing \(2A_{\max}\) (the deciding episode's largest half-amplitude) is not larger than SECULAR_PHASE_UNCERTAINTY_FACTOR × \(\Delta\phi\) (1, a convention) is not resolved from that error: its turning points may be the phases'. It becomes insufficient_time / phase_unresolved, action review: rerun with SECULAR_PLANET_PHASES=measured (SAVE_PLANETS=all), where the measured modes add nothing (rule phase_unresolved; columns sec_phase_uncertainty_deg, sec_phase_swing_deg, sec_phase_swing_ratio). 5026 \(g+g_2-g_4-g_6\) over 10 Myr: \(2A_{\max}\) = 22° against \(\Delta\phi\) = 34°. The outer modes stray too little to matter: the narrowest \(z_1\)/\(z_2\) libration of the benchmark swings 4.6 times its \(\Delta\phi\) (52773), \(\nu_6\) 78 times.

    The center scale. A slow secular libration often carries faster waves that break its turning points into fragments. When the fine scale shows no libration, the angle is filtered again at the scale of its center, \(W_c\) = LIBRATION_CENTRE_CYCLES × \(P_{\rm cyc}\) = \(3P_{\rm cyc}\); if the center itself librates there (or shows episodes the fine scale lacks), that scale decides (sec_scale = centre). There the shortest libration is \(\max(P_{\min},\ 2W_c)\) and a turning point needs the prominence of a center turn. When \(W_c\) shows no more libration than the fine scale, a secular angle tries the scale of its center's own turns (the fine turning points at the prominence of a center turn, \(W\) = their median period / FILTER_MIN_LIBRATION_CUTOFFS; the MMR second scale, SECULAR_CENTRE_TURNS_SCALE): \(P_{\rm cyc}\) of fast waves sets \(W_c\), and its edge trim of \(W_c\) per end, far above what the slow turns need. 101703 g−2g6+g7 over 20 Myr: \(W_c\) = 3.1 Myr from 1-Myr waves trims 3–17 Myr and loses the turn at 18.2 Myr; the turns give 1.6 Myr and one libration of 12 Myr from 6.3 to 18.25 Myr (resonant, extend).

    A wave on a slower motion is no episode. An accepted episode can be a ripple that the slow motion of the angle carries (SECULAR_SLOW_WAVES; secular.decide.slow_waves). The slow motion around it is \(\sigma_f\) between the turns of the center track that bracket it (or the record's edges), of range \(R\):

    • \(R\) below a turn (a slow swing): the episode is a ripple on it when \(A_{\max} \le\) SECULAR_SLOW_SWING_RIPPLE_RATIO × \(R/2\) (0.5, the MMR ratio, a convention) and the swing carries it: the slow track \(S\) (below) moves inside the episode at least SECULAR_SLOW_SWING_CARRY_RATIO (1/16, a convention) of its fastest rate beside it. A wave at a smooth turn of the slow motion reads at least 1/8 (a parabola centred on the episode: \((D/2)^2\) inside against \((3D/2)^2-(D/2)^2\) on an arm as long again); a libration on a plateau of the slow motion, which moves only beside it, reads the filter's leak and stays an episode (two stationary librations joined by a monotone transition: ≤ 0.04). 101703 g−2g6+g7: 8–19° waves of 1 Myr at the turns of the 12-Myr libration (\(R\) = 315°, carried 0.16 and 0.12); 56700 s+s7−2s6: a 13° ripple on a 322° arc of 20 Myr (0.51), which becomes insufficient_time, extend (possibly a slow libration longer than the 20-Myr record);
    • \(R\) a turn or more (a circulation): a capture stops the circulation, a stall only slows it. The slow track \(S\) (\(\sigma\) low-passed at LIBRATION_CENTRE_CYCLES × the episode's period) must carry the angle out of the episode's band beside it (\(\ge A_{\max}\) over as long again before or after; an arm the record cuts below SECULAR_STALL_ARM_MIN_FRACTION of the episode's span is not read) and keep inside the episode at least SECULAR_STALL_RATE_RATIO (0.5, a convention) of that rate. 348018 g−g5+s−s7: a 4° ripple on 4.4 turns, 0.73: non_resonant; the captures of 19289 and 278067 s−s6 read 0.04–0.05, 455327 s−s3 0.24.

    A record-covering episode is never a wave (no motion beside it). The rule runs at every scale (a ripple filtered at the center scale is still one), and the exit and entry are read again on the episodes left.

    Exit and entry. As for an MMR (Geometry), an arc of more than a turn is no piece of libration: when \(\sigma_f\) runs at least LIBRATION_EDGE_ARC_REV = 1 turn monotonically after the last turning point (before the first), the exit (entry) is observed, that end is not covered however short the gap, the angle is episodic and final_regime is a circulation from the exit. A secular angle has no orbit to average, so \(\sigma_f\) itself is read, against the episode's mean center; the center scale is then not tried. 2012 AX10 and 33325 in 2s − s4 − s6 over 20 Myr leave their libration near 16 Myr and run 1.9 and 1.7 turns to the end: transient (the end gap of 0.8 P does not count as covered). An exit that runs less than a turn stays exit_suspected.

    The response of \(e\) and \(i\). To leading order in \(e\) and \(i\), a secular term \(K\,e^{\lvert p\rvert}\sin^{\lvert q\rvert}(i/2)\cos\sigma\) of the averaged disturbing function drives \(\dot e \propto p\sin\sigma\) and \(\frac{d}{dt}\sin\frac i2 \propto q\sin\sigma\) (Lagrange's equations; Murray & Dermott 1999, eqs. 6.146 and 6.150, pp. 251–252). At finite inclination the equation for \(i\) also contains the perihelion derivative, so even \(q = 0\) need not give a zero response of \(\sin\frac i2\). The package fits \(\dot x = A\sin\sigma_f + B\cos\sigma_f + C\) for \(x = e\) and/or \(x = \sin\frac i2\), whichever the angle contains, with free amplitudes: a phase and shape diagnostic that does not enforce the theoretical coefficient. It is reported as check_secular_e_response (pass at \(R^2 \ge\) SECULAR_RESPONSE_R2_MIN = 0.5), a column only: it changes no status and no confidence.

    How long to integrate. An uncertain verdict names the next check in extend_to_years: the baseline \(\tfrac32 P + 2P_c\) with \(P\) at least the shortest libration (500 kyr for a secular angle at the defaults), capped at four and at least twice the record; for ZLK the free-ω gate's cycles or frequency resolution, or review when no length would help (How long to extend). Before a run, the dry-run warns about a record shorter than that baseline (Dry-run).

    Examples: 1222 in \(\nu_6\), 1020 in \(z_1\), 2306 one unfinished slow swing, 847 circulates, 363 too short.

    The Lidov-Kozai angle¶

    What should librate? For a highly inclined orbit perturbed by a distant planet, the quadrupole problem makes the argument of perihelion \(\omega\) librate about 90° or 270° (Lidov's integrals). The package reports \(c_1\), \(c_2\) and \(h_{\rm quad}\) as diagnostics only; no threshold on them enters the status.

    From which plane? The quadrupole problem's reference plane is the perturbers' orbital plane; the package measures \(\omega\), \(i\) and \(\Omega\) from the invariable plane (ZLK_ANGLE_FRAME, default invariable; ecliptic is kept for comparison), by rotating the orbit's angular momentum \(\mathbf h\) and perihelion vector \(\mathbf P\) exactly, never by subtracting angles. The free elements of a ZLK body, the gate, the attribution, the node lock, the e–i coupling and the figure use the same frame. The ecliptic is tilted by \(I = 1.58°\) from that plane: the ecliptic node of an orbit with \(i_{\rm inv} < I\) cannot turn (the orbit's pole circles the invariable pole without enclosing the ecliptic pole), so it librates about the plane's node, and with a bounded \(\varpi\) the ecliptic \(\omega\) looks confined while the invariable one circulates (555527, \(i_{\rm inv} \approx 1.6°\): non_resonant / circulation / certain on both records). At \(i \gtrsim 20°\) the two frames give the same number of turns. Rule zlk_invariable_frame marks a row whose two \(\omega\) part by a whole turn or more (zlk_frame_shift_turns).

    The geometry of \(\omega\) is that of a secular angle with \(P_c = \max(F_{\rm GI} P_{\rm GI},\ P_{\min}/f_{\min})\) and no forced waves: \(F_{\rm GI}\) = ZLK_FILTER_GI_FACTOR = 2, \(P_{\min}\) = ZLK_LIBRATION_PERIOD_MIN_YR = 2 kyr, the prominence from ZLK_TP_PROMINENCE_DEG and ZLK_TP_RIPPLE_FACTOR. The shortest libration is then \(\max(P_{\min},\ 2P_c)\) = 3748 yr, not 2 kyr.

    Why a bounded \(\omega\) is not enough¶

    The osculating \(\omega\) can be bounded without any Lidov-Kozai dynamics. When the forced eccentricity vector is longer than the free one, the point \((e\cos\varpi, e\sin\varpi)\) circles around the tip of the forced vector without enclosing the origin, so \(\varpi\) follows the forced vector's phase instead of turning on its own. The forced vector itself turns at the planets' frequencies, so this does not fix \(\varpi\); but when its phase and the node move together, \(\omega = \varpi - \Omega\) stays confined by the forcing alone. A true ZLK libration lives in the body's free elements, which is why the gate tests the free perihelion against the free node.

    The free-\(\omega\) gate removes the forced parts of \(z_e\) and \(z_i\) at the planetary fundamentals and asks whether \(\omega_{\rm free} = \arg z_{e,\rm free} - \arg z_{i,\rm free}\) stays within a turn. Its outcome is confirmed (confined), kinematic (the free \(\omega\) winds) or no verdict (short_window, undefined_phase, planetary_residual, unavailable); the conditions are in Free elements: the gate. It judges every \(\omega\) that stays within a turn (libration, episodic, drift, insufficient_time).

    From the outcome to the status (decide_status, check check_zlk_free_omega):

    angle_status confirmed kinematic no verdict
    libration resonant / dynamic_libration (check pass) non_resonant / kinematic_libration, origin kinematic, detail free_omega_winds (check fail) uncertain / gate_unavailable, origin unknown
    episodic unchanged (also when confirmed inside its episodes, below) non_resonant / kinematic_libration only if \(\rho_{\max} < 1\) or every episode pinned (below), else read inside the episodes transient only if \(e\) and \(i\) trade at constant \(H\) inside the deciding episode, else uncertain / gate_unavailable (below)
    drift, insufficient_time unchanged non_resonant / kinematic_libration only if \(\rho_{\max} < 1\), else unchanged unchanged

    With FREE_GATE_ENABLED off the outcome is disabled and the geometry's status stands. On a record that does not librate throughout, free elements fitted over the whole record cannot judge a change of regime, so only the strongest case changes it:

    \[ \rho_{\max} = \max_t \frac{\operatorname{rollmed}_W \lvert z_{e,\rm free}\rvert}{\operatorname{rollmed}_W \lvert z_{e,\rm forced}\rvert} < 1, \qquad W = \max\bigl(\tfrac{1}{10}\text{ of the shortest beat of the fitted modes},\ k\,P_{\rm cyc}\bigr), \]

    \(k\) = ZLK_RHO_WINDOW_CYCLES (1, a convention). Each radius is a rolling median over about one libration cycle, so the free radius of every part of the record is compared with the forced one of the same part: a free circle that grows in part of the record (and there encloses the origin) is seen, which one median free radius for the whole record missed. Below one, \(\varpi\) stays tied to the forced phase throughout; the condition is applied only together with a kinematic gate outcome (the free \(\omega\) winds). 555527: 0.66 at 100 kyr, 0.63 at 500 kyr (non_resonant, as the author labeled it). The former median ratio is the column zlk_gate_rho_global.

    An episodic \(\omega\) is also kinematic when every librating episode is the forced vector's: inside each, the median of \(\lvert z_{e,\rm free}\rvert / \lvert z_{e,\rm forced}\rvert\) (instantaneous: a window as long as a forced beat would mix in the surroundings) is below 1, so the free circle stays off the origin there, and over the episode's whole cycles (first turning point to the last of the same kind) the free \(\omega\) advances at least ZLK_EPISODE_FREE_TURNS_PER_CYCLE = 0.5 turns per cycle (a convention: a kinematic cycle is one turn of the free vector about the forced one, since \(P_{\rm cyc}\) is their beat; a free \(\omega\) that librates with the osculating one returns after whole cycles, about 0). An episode the free elements do not measure (outside their baseline, a masked sample in it, fewer than two samples per cycle) pins nothing. Columns zlk_gate_rho_episodes (the largest median) and zlk_gate_free_turns_episodes (the fewest turns per cycle). 624 Hektor: its \(\omega\) circulates but swings back for one to three cycles wherever the free radius dips just below the forced one (0.99 at most inside, 2.0 over the record; 0.94 turns per cycle at least): non_resonant, episodic, kinematic. The real ZLK transients of the benchmark have 3.0–13.7 and at most 0.04.

    The gate inside the episodes (ZLK_GATE_EPISODES). Between its episodes an episodic \(\omega\) circulates, so its free \(\omega\) winds over the record whatever it does inside them, and the record's outcome (kinematic, or a planetary_residual on the winding branch: over 500 kyr some \(g_j - s_k\) always lies within a width of \(b_\omega\)) says nothing about the episodes. Such a row is read inside its librating episodes with the confirmation test: in every one the free vector is the longer one (median \(\rho \ge 1\), column zlk_gate_rho_episodes_min) and the free \(\omega\) advances fewer than ZLK_EPISODE_FREE_TURNS_PER_CYCLE turns per cycle (zlk_gate_free_turns_episodes_max), and \(b_\varpi\), \(b_\Omega\) stay off the \(g\) and \(s\) modes → confirmed, zlk_gate_detail = episodes; a residual at a mode → planetary_residual; an episode the free elements do not measure keeps the record's outcome. 88899 over 500 kyr: 3.0 turns over the record, 0.005 turns per cycle and \(\rho \ge 8.2\) inside both episodes → confirmed; its \(e\)–\(i\) integral is not held there, an objection (transient/low, flag ei_integral_not_held). 1866, 373136, 3752 and 797204 (500 kyr) are confirmed the same way (origin dynamic).

    kinematic_libration is a measurement and gate_unavailable the absence of one: keep them apart (legacy codes). A co-orbital of a planet whose own modes the split does not fit (Mercury to Mars) gets no verdict (unavailable, detail coorbital_unfitted_forcing): uncertain / gate_unavailable whatever bounded \(\omega\) it has, an episodic one, a drift or too few cycles included, since the model does not apply (2020 PP1 and 10563 over 500 kyr; rule zlk_coorbital_episodic). A Jupiter Trojan is covered, its forced vector being Jupiter's g5 + g6 (Free elements).

    An episodic \(\omega\) without a gate verdict (any other no-verdict outcome) needs the physics of a ZLK cycle inside its deciding episode: \(e\) and \(i\) must trade so that the quadrupole integral \(H = \sqrt{1-e^2}\cos i\) stays constant. At the episode's own period \(P\), on its samples only (the whole record also holds stretches where \(\omega\) circulates, which trade \(e\) for \(i\) near \(P\) as well), \(e\), \(i\), \(G = \sqrt{1-e^2}\), \(H\) and \(K = G(1-\cos i)\) are high-passed by a one-period running mean over the whole record and demodulated at \(P\) cycle by cycle; then

    \[ \eta_H = \frac{\operatorname{rms}\,\delta H}{\lvert\cos i\rvert\,\operatorname{rms}\,\delta G} \]

    is 0 when \(H\) is held and 1 as if \(i\) were frozen. The coupling zlk_ei_episode_coupling is zlk when \(i\) is in antiphase with \(e\) (within ZLK_EI_PHASE_TOL_DEG), the demodulated coherence is \(\le -\)ZLK_EI_EPISODE_MIN_COHERENCE and \(\eta_H \le\) ZLK_EI_H_RESIDUAL_MAX (0.5, a convention: the benchmark's ZLK episodes measure at most 0.28, the others at least 0.58); forced_beat the same in phase with \(K\); none; not_measured when the episode spans fewer than ZLK_EI_MIN_CYCLES = 3 periods (two independent estimators agree only by chance below that). In the full tree (v2_verdict) the row stays transient only on zlk, otherwise it is uncertain / gate_unavailable (−9), rule zlk_gate_unconfirmed; the minimal policy reads the same coupling as the e–i integral (below). 373136, 3752 and 797204 over 500 kyr stay transient (\(\eta_H\) 0.05–0.23 over 4.6–8 periods); 88899 over 500 kyr (\(e\) and \(i\) out of antiphase, \(\eta_H\) 1.2) and 797204 over 100 kyr and 2023 RS2 (deciding episodes of 1.5 and 2.4 periods) become uncertain. The record's coupling (zlk_ei_coupling) stays a column.

    Under the minimal policy (the table above is the full tree's, kept in v2_verdict): the quadrupole problem, its integral thresholds (\(h^2 < 3/5\), \(c_1\), Kozai's 39.2°) and its half-turn symmetry hold only for a hierarchical, axisymmetric perturber, outside their domain for main-belt and planet-crossing asteroids (Gronchi & Milani 1999; Gallardo et al. 2012); none of them decides. The geometry of \(\omega\) gives the status (one revolution is \(2\pi\)), and the physics reads two measurements on the deciding episode:

    • the \(e\)–\(i\) integral above, measured on every bounded \(\omega\) (a libration too): broken (measured, not zlk) where the forced terms are small, \(\rho_{\max} \ge\) ZLK_RHO_KINEMATIC_EXCLUDED (3, a convention: a non-resonant forced \(e\) is at most ~0.1), is a veto, non_resonant / kinematic_libration, detail ei_integral_not_held (88899 over 500 kyr, \(\eta_H\) 1.24, \(\rho_{\max}\) 13); with larger forced terms it is an objection (flag ei_integral_not_held, level low), since the forcing itself breaks \(\sqrt{1-e^2}\cos i\);
    • the forced vector: a gate kinematic on a libration is a veto (zlk_kinematic), and so is \(\rho_{\max} < 1\) over the record together with a kinematic gate on an episodic angle, a drift or too few cycles (zlk_free_e); the per-episode pin (a median) and an undefined free phase veto only together with a broken integral (624 Hektor: \(\eta_H\) 1.04), otherwise they are objections. \(\rho_{\max}\) is a ratio of two parts of one split, not a fact independent of it: 591986 refitted with g7 for the unresolved g5 gives 2.9 instead of 0.43, a planetary residual 0.83″/yr from g5 and resonant/low with gate_unavailable; the default split (g5, the one the 4-Myr record resolves) is kinematic.

    A gate without a verdict keeps the geometry's status with the flag gate_unavailable at most medium, except when the integral holds and \(\rho_{\max} \ge\) ZLK_RHO_KINEMATIC_EXCLUDED: the forced vector cannot hold the perihelion, so the abstention (1981 Midas: its free node near \(s_6\)) is no cap and asks no extension (flag kinematic_excluded). Only a confirmed gate names a dynamic origin; otherwise the reason is geometric_libration/geometric_episode.

    The scale. The gate's period is that of the deciding librating episode (the covering one, or the one with the most turning points; sec_decisive_period_yr), and zlk_gate_n_cycles = \(T/P\) counts cycles of the osculating \(\omega\). F, the \(e\)–\(i\) coupling, the \(\rho\) window and the extension read the same \(P\). Without a librating episode it is the median full period of the runs not faster than the floor. The median over every episode (\(P_{\rm cyc}\), geo_period_cycle_yr) is a column only: 2020 PP1's five episodes run from 2.3 to 34 kyr. The attribution of an \(\omega\) pinned inside every episode tests the beat against the episodes' pooled period, since each episode is a cycle of the same beat (624 Hektor). A libration at the center scale whose period is within a Rayleigh width of a beat \(2\pi/|g_k - g_j|\) of two fitted forced modes with nonzero amplitudes, whose reconstructed forced phase wave explains the center (correlation \(\ge\) ZLK_CENTRE_MODEL_R_MIN, swing within a factor ZLK_CENTRE_MODEL_AMP_FACTOR), while \(\rho_{\max} < 1\) at the fine period, is the forced vector turning its direction, not a libration of \(\omega\): the fine scale stays decisive (sec_centre_rejected = forced_beat:<gk>-<gj>). Without the per-mode amplitudes (an old file) there is no measured beat and no rejection. The guard acts only where the gate does: with the gate disabled, without a split or for a co-orbital whose forcing the split does not fit, the geometric scale stands.

    Columns that change no status (Free elements: beyond the gate): the \(e\)–\(i\) coupling of the whole record at the decisive period (zlk_ei_coupling, every row; check_zlk_ei and confirmed_ei are zlk coupling; the deciding episode's own coupling zlk_ei_episode_* is a column too, except for an episodic row without a gate verdict), the attribution of a kinematic libration to a forced mode (zlk_forcing_*), the node lock (zlk_node_lock*) and the slow line of the center (zlk_centre_*, 591986: the g6−g5 beat). The strongest coherent line of the \(e\)–\(i\) cross-spectrum (Cross-spectral coherence) is the columns zlk_coherent, zlk_check.

    Examples: 3040 librates, 591986 kinematic, 162474 over 1.5 Myr, 1981 without a verdict, 15527 circulates, 2010 HY22 unphysical.

    Confidence and output¶

    The level follows the shared rule (Confidence); a suspected exit or entry caps it at medium. A secular angle has no physics margin; for ZLK it is G.

    The margins and their classes

    S = support, D = decisive (Confidence).

    margin what full when
    N (S) turning points of the decisive episode above the minimum (thin at it, with the cap three_tp_episode, ≤ high)
    S (S) its center every cycle's band overlaps, with enough independent cycles; not measured with one; not applicable to a modulated center
    F (S) the filter the libration period ≥ FILTER_MIN_LIBRATION_CUTOFFS × \(P_c\); else not measured (flag filter_too_slow)
    P (D) a circulation its largest center pass ≥ 1 + CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND = 1.1 turns at every smoothing (×0.5, ×1, ×2); else thin
