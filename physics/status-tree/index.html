    The status tree¶

    The geometry (What the angle does) says what \(\sigma\) does over the record: angle_status. The physics (Is it this resonance?) measures whether \(a\) answers \(\sigma\) as the pendulum of this resonance requires. The status tree combines the two, always in the same order, into status, reason and libration_origin. The first question that has an answer decides; the later ones are not asked.

    The order follows one principle: a cause outside this resonance is looked for before the libration is credited to it. A bounded \(\sigma\) is first checked against the sampling (can the record be read at all?), then against other resonances (a member of the same multiplet, another commensurability), then against pure geometry (the perihelion term), and only then is its own force law measured.

    This page describes mean-motion resonances (two-body, three-body, retrograde, co-orbital). Secular and Lidov-Kozai angles use a shorter tree, given in Secular and Lidov-Kozai.

    Minimal policy (default)¶

    The tree below runs first and measures everything; a policy layer (resonance/analysis/policy.py) then re-reads the finished analysis by a few principles:

    1. Status from the geometry of \(\sigma_f\). libration → resonant, episodic → transient, near_separatrix → near_separatrix, circulation → non_resonant. A drift whose slow center passes ≥ one own revolution → non_resonant/modulated_circulation, less → uncertain. insufficient_time → uncertain, or non_resonant/circulation when the record ends in circulation (\(\sigma_f\) travels ≥ one own revolution after the last turning point of an accepted or candidate run; for an MMR, of any run, accepted or rejected, or candidate span in the window, one that runs past the window's end counting at that end; a secular or ZLK travel below \(1 +\) CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND turns holds the level at medium). One own revolution is \(2\pi\), for a ZLK \(\omega\) too (the half-turn symmetry of the quadrupole's \(\cos 2\omega\) is not general; ZLK runs still break at half a turn). The geometry alone names no mechanism: a secular or ZLK libration is geometric_libration/geometric_episode, origin unknown, unless a confirmed ZLK gate makes it dynamic.
    2. Only a measured contradiction vetoes a bounded angle (column veto): an owner that induces the episode (step 2), a kinematic origin (step 3, K2, K3s), a two-body strength from the disturbing function far below the need (5s; the angle's word is then circulation or drift: no run is its libration), every counted episode below 45° being the forced wave its background carries (forced_wave: at the episode's own period the envelopes of \(\sigma\) and \(a\) inside stay within the band the same steady wave shows within MMR_FORCED_WAVE_REFERENCE_PERIODS periods around it; a libration adds its own swing; some such episodes leave the count; 2012 KL9; runs found forced join the background of the others; when the slow center passes less than a revolution the drift rule decides instead: uncertain / drift), the ZLK gate measured kinematic on a libration, the free \(e\) below the forced \(e\) over the record (zlk_free_e; inside every episode or with an undefined free phase only when the \(e\)–\(i\) integral is broken too), or the \(e\)–\(i\) exchange of the deciding ZLK episode breaking \(\sqrt{1-e^2}\cos i\) where the forced terms are small, \(\rho_{\max} \ge\) ZLK_RHO_KINEMATIC_EXCLUDED (zlk_integral; with larger forced terms it is an objection, ei_integral_not_held; Secular and ZLK). Every other leaf that moved a status (5a, 5b, 5e, 5f, 5g, 5o, 5x, 5n, 5s three-body, 5s*, 2j, a ZLK gate without a verdict) gives the geometry's status back with a flag: measured_objection and strength_scale_implausible set the level to low, gate_unavailable/gate_disabled to at most medium (not when the integral holds and \(\rho_{\max} \ge\) ZLK_RHO_KINEMATIC_EXCLUDED: flag kinematic_excluded, no cap, no extension). A veto within CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND of its threshold still moves the status, at medium (veto_thin).
    3. The level from the evidence (Confidence); no caps.
    4. Everything else is a flag: every cap of the full tree, zone, owner, subresonance and edge mark. The alias (C5) and the undersampled step stay.
    5. Extend only for an unfinished libration at the record's end (an end-edge 1:1 leg A1, an end-edge half-swing A2, insufficient_time or a last counted episode at the end with fewer than CONFIDENCE_HIGH_CYCLES cycles A), a deciding period above \(2T/\)LIBRATION_MIN_TURNING_POINTS (B), or a needed veto not measured for lack of length (flag veto_needs_length: MMR L4 short of force windows, a ZLK gate short_window/planetary_residual) whose record pol_veto_need_yr is within the next-check budget MMR_NEXT_CHECK_SPAN_FACTOR\(\,T\) (C, extend to that record). Never when the record ends in circulation or the level is certain (a certain row asks for nothing: action none) (column pol_extend_clause). A needed veto beyond the budget keeps the flag and goes to review. The length asked for is the next check of the deciding period's missing cycles, at least of the baseline's estimate when that is a slow swing seen in part (base_period_source arc_slow, b5, two_omega, modulation: its slow cycles), within MMR_NEXT_CHECK_SPAN_FACTOR\(\,T\) (523683 2U-9: 400 kyr, three slow cycles of 112 kyr).
    6. Episodes: the figure and n_counted_episodes show only the counted episodes; candidates are drawn faint. One clean 1:1 leg (G4c) is a transient of half a cycle; a half-swing between passages (G4h) is a candidate of a circulation; an end-edge one extends.
    7. ZLK and secular follow the same rules; a secular swing below the mode-phase uncertainty stays uncertain/phase_unresolved (review), never non_resonant.

    The verdict of the full tree stays in v2_verdict (status/confidence/reason/action/angle_status); pol_decided_by names the step that gave the final status (tree when the policy kept the tree's, ;not_upheld:<leaf> when it did not uphold the leaf's veto) and pol_objection_check the checks behind a measured objection (Output).

    Before the tree: can the record be read?¶

    These checks come before any geometry. A record that fails one of them gets status = invalid: the run says nothing about the resonance at these settings, and the action says how to repair it.

    question test reason action
    Is the angle a resonance of this body at all? the coefficient of the body's \(\lambda\) is \(k \ne 0\) invalid_input review
    Is the orbit an ellipse? \(a > 0\), \(0 \le e < 1\), all values finite unphysical review
    Can the filter keep the shortest libration? \(P_c > 2\,\Delta t\) (signal) undersampled resample
    Can the angle be unwrapped without guessing turns? unwrap residuals at most MMR_UNWRAP_MAX_RESID_P99_DEG = 45° (99th percentile) and MMR_UNWRAP_MAX_RESID_DEG = 135° (largest) alias (angle_status = undefined) resample (review when no finer step helps)
    Is \(\varpi\) defined? (very small \(e\)) the \(p\varpi\) part jitters by more than MMR_JITTER_MAX_RAD = 0.785 rad, the full angle cannot be unwrapped, and \(\varpi\) is undefined over more than MMR_VARPI_UNDEFINED_MAX_SHARE = 0.5 of the record undefined_phase review

    Steps in order¶

    The tree reads the angle as the status reads it: a record that librates in episodes and then circulates, or librates before a jump of \(a\), or is captured after one, is judged as the episodic angle its episodes show. The reported angle_status may then differ from the word the tree read; see below. The episodes the tree counts are the geometry's: for an angle that is not 1:1, a center step or a stall between two accepted pieces in one libration region is a mark of one episode, not a boundary (rule regime_cut_mark, geometry); a single half-libration between circulations is G4h below.

    G6. Too short. angle_status = insufficient_time (too few turning points, an arc, a stall): uncertain, baseline_needed, action extend (Confidence gives the length asked for); action review when the period that decides already fits the record (base_cause): more time would decide nothing.

    A ripple on an unfinished slow swing (rule ripple_on_arc, MMR_RIPPLE_ON_ARC) does not stop G6. An accepted episode is set aside before G2, as an unconfirmed ripple (forced_wave = unconfirmed_ripple), when its half-amplitude stays below MMR_A_RESPONSE_MIN_AMP_DEG = 45° (there neither the force law nor \(V_E pprox V_F\) can be measured), its identity does not pass (fails, or cannot be measured on so few cycles: nothing confirms it), and the slow center turns back once or twice within one revolution (fewer than three turns: no full slow cycle). A record-covering episode and a 1:1 angle are never set aside; a real small libration keeps its passing identity. G6 then counts only the turning points outside the ripples; with none the slow center decides: one turn → the G5a arc, else arc or stall by its range. 2004 YG1 9M-10J-4 over 20 kyr: a 3° ripple at the turn of a 281° slow swing → uncertain / insufficient_time (arc), extend to 50 kyr; 2002 BG 1E-10S-1 over 20 kyr: a 2° 735-yr ripple (identity not measured) at the one turn of a 1.68-rad slow swing → the same, extend to 50 kyr (the slow swing's baseline; over 100 kyr its center drifts 1.6 revolutions: non_resonant).

    A slow swing in the resonance's own well (rule G5w, slow_swing_in_well, MMR_SLOW_SWING_IN_WELL). A drift needs a center that keeps going: G5a already reads one slow turn back as an arc. With two turns the record holds one half-swing between them, and the labeled drifts of 101703 and 142694 1V+3S-7 have two turns too, so the turns alone decide nothing. The half-swing reads as part of a slow libration of this resonance, insufficient_time / arc (instead of drift, or of few_cycles for the fast turning points riding on it), when it obeys the resonance's pendulum: the identity \(\dot\sigma_s = c\,(a_s - a_*)\) holds on σ and \(a\) low-passed at W_c, and between the turns both the \(a\) swing (one side: \(a_*\) to \(a_* + 2\omega_0\sin(A/2)/|c|\)) and the period \(2(t_2-t_1)\) agree with the directly averaged well at the half-swing's amplitude \(A\) (\(T_{0,\text{th}}\), \(T = T_{0,\text{th}}F(A)\), \(F = \tfrac{2}{\pi}K(\sin^2\tfrac{A}{2})\)) within \([1/\sqrt{x}, \sqrt{x}]\), \(x\) = MMR_STRENGTH_CHECK_RATIO (no new threshold). Anything unmeasured (three-body: no direct theory; 1:1; one or three turns) leaves the row as it was. The extension asks for MMR_EXTEND_SLOW_CYCLES = 3 slow periods (a budget). 523683 2U-9 over 100 kyr (its 379-yr cycles are its own orbital period, cut off by the cutoff ≥ 1.5 \(P_\text{orb}\)): turns at 11.9 and 67.7 kyr, \(A\) = 114°, identity 1.00, \(a\) swing 1.07 ×, period 1.00 × → uncertain / insufficient_time, extend to 400 kyr. 101703 and 142694 fail the slow identity (slope/c −0.27, −0.25) and stay drift.

    A cycle faster than the floor (rule G1f, MMR_SUBFLOOR_PASS). The 150-yr floor stays, but a two-body angle (not 1:1) without a libration over the record is analyzed once more at the floor of its own averaged potential, \(\max(T_{0,\text{th}}/\sqrt{x},\ 2\Delta t \cdot\) FILTER_MIN_LIBRATION_CUTOFFS\()\), \(x\) = MMR_STRENGTH_CHECK_RATIO, when that is below the floor. \(T_{0,\text{th}}\) is the shortest small-amplitude period of the directly averaged potential at the snapshots whose swings lie in its well (no episode needed; a pendulum only gets slower with amplitude). The pass replaces the verdict only when its decisive cycle is faster than the floor, F passes by direct averaging, the theoretical center lies inside every observed swing and L4 passes; otherwise the first verdict stays (columns geo_subfloor_*). The cap short_period_aliased keeps reading the 150-yr floor; three-body angles have no computed theory and are never eligible (the forced 115–121-yr waves of 52356 and 23240). 2001 VC2 16E-17: \(T_{0,\text{th}}\) = 112 yr, a 105-yr libration over 20 kyr → resonant / libration / medium (the alias cap).

    C. Circulation. The center of \(\sigma\) runs through a full turn.

    • If the sampled angle is an alias (the true rate \(\nu\) folded by the output step), the record can still exclude a libration when the alias branch is the same over the whole range of \(a\) and the body is far from the resonance: the far ratio (the distance \(|\langle a\rangle - a_*|\) over half the \(a\)-range, or over the offset of the \(a\) implied by the measured mean motion when that is larger; signal) is at least MMR_C5_FAR_RATIO_MIN = 3 (rule C5). Then non_resonant / alias; otherwise a libration may hide behind the step: invalid / alias, action resample.
    • If a member of the multiplet has a librating verdict of its own, the row takes the member's status (as in step 1 below).
    • If \(\sigma\) turns back at times but the energy measured from \(a\) does not follow a potential of \(\sigma\) (R0 fails): non_resonant / no_resonant_potential.
    • G4c, one 1:1 leg (MMR_COORBITAL_SINGLE_LEG). A co-orbital angle without a librating episode is judged on its single legs: the stretches of the orbit-averaged angle between two reversals, outside every accepted run, that do not sweep a turn and whose period \(2L\) is at least the floor less CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND. Each leg is read against the averaged potential (Local force and energy on a single leg): is it one swing of a co-orbital well (tadpole, horseshoe, quasi-satellite, not a lap past the planet), does its local 1:1 law hold with \(K > 0\), is its energy conserved, and is each turn a reflection (\(|\ln(\text{out}/\text{in})| \le\) MMR_COORBITAL_TURN_ENERGY_TOL, zone \(\le\) MMR_COORBITAL_TURN_ZONE_MAX)?
    • everything holds → transient / dynamic_episode, cap one_coorbital_leg (≤ medium): 2023 OE5, 586–665 yr;
    • one discrepancy → uncertain / one_coorbital_leg, action review (2018 KS: a lap past the Earth at 779–934 yr);
    • a clean leg cut by the record edge whose observed turn reflects → uncertain / baseline_needed, angle word insufficient_time, action extend;
    • both turns are kicks → the circulation below, with the cap near_episode (1991 VG: out/in 0.61 and 1.59); a turn that would reflect but for a zone within CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND above MMR_COORBITAL_TURN_ZONE_MAX is no kick: uncertain / one_coorbital_leg, review (2020 FN3: zones 1.11 and 1.15 against 1.1);
    • no leg at all (one reflection between passages, 2008 EZ84) → the circulation below, unchanged.

    The strongest outcome among the legs decides (transient > uncertain > extend > near-episode). The legs of an accepted episode are never read again: its turns already closed a cycle. MMR_COORBITAL_LEG_FORCE_GATE (off) reads a failed 1:1 law as a kick instead of a discrepancy. - G4h, one half-swing of another resonance (MMR_SINGLE_HALF_LIBRATION, not 1:1). A bounded half-swing between two passages over the top (or a passage and the record edge), at least the floor less the thin band long, that follows the resonance's own one-harmonic law (\(\dot a = A\sin\sigma + B\cos\sigma + C\) with \(R^2 \ge\) MMR_FORCE_R2_FAIL on each half of its window, the force turning \(\sigma\) back at both turns) → uncertain / one_half_libration (review); at the record edge uncertain / baseline_needed, extend (42946 43S-6). A slow wide turn of a circulation does not follow that law and stays a circulation. - Otherwise non_resonant / circulation; an owner found for a circulating angle only names its origin.

    1. Is a member of the multiplet librating instead? A resonance \(k\lambda + \sum k_j\lambda_j + p\varpi + \dots\) comes with members that differ by \(m(\varpi_P - \varpi)\) or by node terms (Morbidelli 2002, §9.3–9.4). If one member has a fixed center while \(\sigma\)'s center moves with \(\varpi_P - \varpi\), the libration belongs to that member: the catalog row takes the member's own status (an invalid member makes it uncertain), reason = subresonance_librates, origin subresonance, owner = the member, and also the member's confidence (Confidence); its angle_status stays its own. An accepted member without a verdict of its own gives non_resonant / subresonance_librates. When the catalog angle and the member both librate over the record, each keeps its own verdict. Example: 82831 13M-24.

    2. Does another resonance drive \(a\)? The owner search looks for another commensurability \(\phi_o\) whose force explains \(\dot a\) better than \(\sigma\)'s own term. Since \(\dot\sigma \simeq c\,(a - a_*)\) holds for any oscillation of \(a\), an \(a\) driven by \(\phi_o\) makes \(\sigma\) oscillate too: an induced libration. The owner is accepted only if its intervals cover at least MMR_OWNER_COVER_MIN = 0.5 of \(\sigma\)'s decisive episode; then non_resonant / induced_libration, origin induced. The detail says whether the owner librates (owner_libration) or circulates at the oscillation's period (owner_forced_wave). Examples: 117306, 1997 GK3. The exception: when the remainder \(x = \Psi/u\) closes cycles of a well of its own ('both resonances at once?' confirmed, Response), the tree goes on and the row keeps its own reading with origin joint; one such cycle gives uncertain / baseline_needed, origin joint.

    3. Does \(a\) carry the oscillation at all? In a resonance the swing of \(a\) follows from the swing of \(\sigma\): \(\delta a \simeq \delta\dot\sigma/|c|\). If \(a\) carries less than MMR_B1_RATIO_MIN = 0.1 of that (test B1), or the cycle runs at a secular period of the body (the \(e\)-cycle \(2\pi/|g - g_5|\) or the \(2\omega\) cycle, within MMR_KIN_PERIOD_TOL = 0.1) while the identity and every force law fail, the bound comes from the geometry of the angle (its \(p\varpi\) part, a secular cycle): non_resonant / kinematic_libration, origin kinematic. When the slow center itself follows the \(\lambda\) part plus the secular part (correlation of rates ≥ MMR_KIN_CORR_MIN = 0.9, over at least MMR_KIN_MIN_POINTS = 3 independent points), \(a\) does not answer (every force law fails, measured on at least MMR_FORCE_MIN_WINDOWS = 2 windows) and the librating stretches are only a ripple on it (their swing below MMR_KIN_RIPPLE_SHARE_MAX = 0.5 of the center's range), or a kinematic libration's center moves more than MMR_KIN_DRIFT_SHIFT_RATIO = 1 amplitude: non_resonant / kinematic_drift. If a libration slower than the record cannot be excluded and the center's rate is consistent with an exact commensurability (within MMR_B5_EXACT_SIGMA = 2 standard errors, B5): uncertain / baseline_needed. The kinematic drift is rule K2 (rec_rule 3k2), the exact commensurability rec_rule 3b5. Examples: 285637, 62243.

    K3s, after the leaf: a circulation whose slow scale is a secular cycle. A non_resonant / circulation (no mechanism named, not 1:1) whose slow scale has the period of a secular cycle (within MMR_KIN_PERIOD_TOL) and fails its own identity becomes non_resonant / kinematic_libration, origin kinematic, detail secular_cycle (rec_rule K3s, MMR_SECULAR_CYCLE_KINEMATIC), whatever B1 or a thin L4. Only where the slow libration is the record's regime: the center leaves it by slips of one turn (largest monotone pass ≤ 1 + LIBRATION_SLIP_TOLERANCE rev); a center that circulates several turns in a row stays a circulation (285637 2 Myr, 4.7 rev). The status and the level stay those of the circulation; a librating row is never demoted. Example: 37087 3J+8S-3 (100 kyr and 1 Myr).

    4. A drift without a cause. angle_status = drift with no kinematic explanation: uncertain / drift_mechanism_unknown. Example: 142694.

    5. Is the libration this resonance's own force? Three measurements can show it (Is it this resonance?): the force law L4 (\(\dot a\) follows \(\sin(\sigma - \varphi_0)\)), the potential R0 (the energy \(\tfrac12\dot\sigma^2 + V(\sigma)\) built from \(a\) is conserved cycle by cycle) and the strength F (the force needed for the observed period against the force the disturbing function gives). Their combinations:

    leaf measurements status / reason origin
    5f L4 cannot be told from a forced line of the angle's lattice at the same period, and the \(\sigma\)-only potential test does not fail uncertain / origin_unknown (flag force_law_contaminated) unknown
    5s L4 and R0 do not pass, and the period needs ≥ MMR_STRENGTH_IMPLAUSIBLE_RATIO = \(10^3\) × the strength computed from the disturbing function (two-body: direct or harmonic, not centre_outside_well unless MMR_STRENGTH_FIRST_OUTSIDE_WELL; three-body: \(\mu_i\mu_j e^q\) with the small-divisor residual still ≥ \(10^3\)); cap ≤ low non_resonant / implausible_strength unknown
    5a L4 fails, R0 does not pass (5a* when R0 is not measured rather than failed) uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5b L4 passes, R0 fails uncertain / origin_unknown (flag force_law_disagrees) unknown
    5e R0 fails, L4 not measured uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5g L4 and R0 not measured, the identity fails on an episode and passes on none uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5c → 6 L4 fails, R0 passes by the behavior (below), cap force_law_disagrees dynamic
    5d → 6 L4 passes and R0 does not fail; or R0 passes and L4 is not measured; or (a near_separatrix angle) neither is measured and nothing objects by the behavior (below) dynamic

    A failed \(\sigma\)-only potential test (period law or fixed well, no \(\dot a\) in it) stays an objection even when L4 is contaminated: the tree then skips 5f and goes on through 5s and the other leaves.

    For an angle of the near_separatrix type, the leaves 5a, 5e and 5g give non_resonant instead: no_resonant_potential, or modulated_circulation when the angle only slows down periodically (pattern B).

    On an output step that folds short periods into slow ones, a failed L4 or identity is read as not measured when the directly averaged strength F passes and the short-period scatter of \(a\) exceeds MMR_SHORT_PERIOD_A_RATIO = 0.5 of the libration's half-swing in \(a\): the folded terms, not the dynamics, spoil the fit.

    The unmeasured branch. When neither L4 nor R0 could be measured (a small amplitude, one force window) and \(\sigma\) librates, the first leaf that holds:

    leaf condition status / reason origin
    5d → 6 the strength computed by direct averaging passes, required / theory within [1/x, x], x = MMR_STRENGTH_CHECK_RATIO = 10 by the behavior dynamic
    5s the period needs ≥ \(10^3\) × the strength (two-body: direct averaging; three-body: \(\mu_i\mu_j e^q\) even after the small-divisor envelope) non_resonant / implausible_strength unknown
    5s* three-body ratio ≥ \(10^3\), but the envelope could close the gap uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5o a measured objection: an episodic angle, whose episodes do not lie in the separatrix zone, with a strong \(a\)-wave already before the episode (O1 ≥ MMR_O1_RATIO_FAIL = 0.5, O1); or F by direct averaging fails uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5x the order-of-magnitude strength (\(\mu_i\mu_j e^q\), three-body and 1:1) lies outside [1/x, x]: a scale known to a factor 1–100 is a diagnostic, no objection; origin not established (79653 3M+3J-7: 39.5 ×) uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5n the decisive episode has fewer than LIBRATION_CENTRE_MIN_ESTIMATES = 2 independent cycles uncertain / origin_unknown unknown
    5u → 6 nothing measured by the behavior, reason geometric_libration / geometric_episode, cap ≤ low (geometry_only) or ≤ medium (strength_scale_only, when the order-of-magnitude strength agrees) unknown

    Two kinds of uncertain / origin_unknown leaves: a measurement contradicts the angle's own dynamics (5a, 5b, 5e's failed R0, 5g's failed identity, 5o), or nothing was measured (5n, 5x). Where the only missing measurement is missing for lack of length, the action is extend, not review (Actions).

    6. By the observed behavior. When the force is measured, or nothing objects, the angle's own behavior gives the status:

    angle (as the tree reads it) status reason (force measured) reason (geometry only, 5u)
    libration resonant dynamic_libration geometric_libration
    episodic transient dynamic_episode geometric_episode
    near_separatrix near_separatrix separatrix_zone — (the branch applies to librating angles only)

    The separatrix zone and the energies of the cycles add flags here (libration_in_separatrix_zone, episodes_in_separatrix_zone) and confidence evidence; the zone never relabels a geometric libration as near_separatrix. It has one indirect effect on the status: episodes in the zone bypass the O1 objection (leaf 5o) of the unmeasured branch.

    6u. A union of episodes. Librating episodes that together cover the record, with no circulation between them, are read first as episodic. Only if that reading reaches transient / dynamic_episode with a measured force (leaf 6, origin dynamic) is the record promoted to resonant / dynamic_libration (rec_rule gets the suffix u: 5d/6u). Every other outcome stays as it is. (For a co-orbital 1:1 angle the union is a libration directly: its episodes are the tadpole, horseshoe and quasi-satellite regimes of one resonance.)

    After the tree. A non-resonant verdict next to a run of turning points one turn short of an episode (LIBRATION_NEAR_EPISODE_TP_DEFICIT = 1) and at least the floor less CONFIDENCE_THIN_BAND long gets the flag and cap near_episode; for a 1:1 angle such a run may also be one rejected over the top, when each of its half-swings crosses at most one of the planet and L3 (rule coorbital_near_episode); an owner search run on catalog planet longitudes caps the level (catalogue_longitudes). These change the confidence, not the status.

    The reported angle word¶

    The tree reads the angle as the status needs it; the reported angle_status describes what \(\sigma\) did over the whole record. They differ in four cases:

    • Episodes, then another regime. Librating episodes followed by a circulation, by a regime after a jump of \(a\), or a capture after a jump: reported episodic (recurrent with two or more episodes); how the record ends goes to final_regime (circulation, libration, …).
    • A union of episodes. Episodes covering the record together are reported libration (subtype regime_change) whatever the status: \(\sigma\) never went round.
    • A kinematic drift of the slow center. The word follows the mechanism, not the ripple: drift, or circulation when the center's largest monotone pass reaches a full turn or its net shift reaches LIBRATION_CIRCULATION_NET_REV = 2 turns (MMR_KINEMATIC_DRIFT_WORD = True).
    • Forced waves. Runs that are forced waves of another argument are not episodes for the status. If the record would otherwise be reported circulation or drift while \(\sigma\) loops in those runs, the librating word is reported.
    • Forced (strength). On a row decided by 5s (not 5s*) every accepted run is marked a forced wave of route strength once the verdict is made (MMR_FORCED_WAVE_STRENGTH): its period needs a force ≥ \(10^3\) times the one the disturbing function gives, so it is no libration of this angle. The mark names that ground, not a driver; nothing is measured again, and the status, word, reason and level stay as they are.

    Only the output changes; the margins and the confidence read the angle as the tree did.

    How the record's edges end¶

    After the tree, the MMR verdict reads how \(\sigma_f\) meets the record's edges (rule end_arc_extend, MMR_RECORD_EDGE_STRETCH). The edge pass is the stretch from the slow center's turn nearest the edge (or the other edge without one) to the edge.

    • End arc after circulation. The pass holds at least one revolution, and after its last completed one \(\sigma_f\) stays \(D\) to the end without completing another, with \(D >\) MMR_END_ARC_SLOWDOWN_FACTOR (2) × the pass's longest revolution and \(2D \ge\) MMR_END_ARC_MIN_FLOOR_PERIODS (1) × the floor, no librating episode in it. A circulation whose rate depends on \(\sigma\) alone completes the next revolution sooner than a full one takes, so this is a slowdown. final_regime becomes arc (range ≥ π) or stall from the last revolution, when it was empty or circulation, and the flag extend_time is set forward. A non_resonant / circulation verdict there gets the cap record_ends_unresolved (≤ medium): the record ends before it shows whether \(\sigma\) circulates on or is captured (2022 BB2: 600 yr after 52-yr revolutions).
    • Flag only. The same slowdown at the start, one reflection of the slow center cut by an edge (less than a revolution between the turn and the edge, at least one beyond it: 2008 EZ84 before 144 yr), a clean 1:1 edge leg whose turn reflects (G4c's extend) and an edge half-libration that holds its law (G4h) set extend_time with the direction of their edge (backward at the start, forward at the end, both); a window that starts at a jump of \(a\) has no record start to extend. One reflection is no cycle: no cap.

    The flag changes no status and no level, whatever the action. extend_direction gives the direction and extend_to_years the length: the next check without an estimate, round_up(2T) (with action extend the action's length stays). The words libration, drift, insufficient_time and undefined already say how the record ends: no edge stretch is read under them.

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai angles¶

    Secular and Lidov-Kozai angles use their own shorter tree, without an owner, a subresonance or a force law step: geometry and status and, for Lidov-Kozai, the free-ω gate. A secular libration makes only a few cycles in a record, so which runs are its episodes is read as for an MMR but with four rules of its own (IDs G2p, G2t, G2w, G2e in the rule index): each run of turning points takes its own prominence; a bounded run whose center turns back once is modulated; an episode that is a wave on a slower motion of the angle does not count (secular only; the slower scale decides); and \(\sigma_f\) running a turn or more after the last turning point is an observed exit (transient, final_regime circulation), not a libration that goes on past the record.
