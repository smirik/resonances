    Plots¶

    Which figures a simulation draws is set by plots (default ['combined'], the one-page verdict figure). Which rows get them is set by plot (a filter on the verdict, see Configuration) and plot_always. The default review draws every row except a confident plain circulation: the nonzero rows and the doubtful negatives (non_resonant at medium or low confidence, the review queue review_required). After the run the log prints the rows by status × confidence and how many were drawn.

    sim = resonances.Simulation(
    plot='nonzero',
    plots=['evolution', 'combined'],
    plot_options={'style': 'screen'},  # optional, see below
)
    kind resonances file ({body}-{key}-…) what it shows
    combined MMR, secular, ZLK combined/…-combined the one-page verdict figure: time panels left, the verdict and every number of the decision right
    evolution all {body}-{key}.png angle, a, e, i against time, periodograms against period (PLOT_CONFIG preset; a low-passed by the body-level filter, LIBRATION_FILTER_CUTOFF)
    recurrence MMR -recurrence recurrence of the state \((\sigma, \dot\sigma)\)
    fair prograde two-body MMR -fair FAIR plane: mean anomaly against the longitude difference to the planet
    portrait MMR, secular, ZLK -portrait phase portrait: \(\sigma\)–\(\dot\sigma\) (or \(\sigma\)–a) for an MMR, \((r\cos\sigma, r\sin\sigma)\) for a secular or ZLK angle
    angle secular, ZLK -angle \(\sigma(t)\): osculating, filtered, turning points
    rate secular, ZLK -rate the rate of the filtered \(\sigma\), with the divisor
    cycles MMR -cycles oscillation cycles of \(\sigma_f\) and of a (the analysis' \(a_f\), else the osculating a), stacked on one period
    ecc_vector all {body}-ecc-vector.png the \((k, h) = (e\cos\omega, e\sin\omega)\) plane
    cross_spectrum all -coherence-{pair} coherence and cross-phase (Cross-spectral coherence)
    free_omega Lidov-Kozai -free-omega-drift, -portrait, -vector the free argument of pericentre (Free elements)
    clones all, a body with clones {body}-{key}-clones.png the ensemble: every clone's angle by status, status against initial a (with the catalog \(a_{\rm res}\) of an MMR), the status shares with Wilson intervals, or 'no shares' for an unusable covariance (also drawn with combined; Clones)

    An unknown name is an error; a kind is drawn only for the resonance types it lists. The image format is image_type (png or pdf). Combined figures go to plot_path/combined/, the others to plot_path/figures/, both split by plot_layout (confidence/status by default: combined/high/resonant/…; Output data). None of these figures feeds a status.

    What every panel, line and icon means: How to read the figures, with an example of each kind. One example for every status: Gallery.

    Colors and units¶

    One palette gives a quantity the same color in every panel of the combined figures, MMR and secular/ZLK alike:

    color what
    black a filtered series
    gray the osculating series (a band when the samples are dense)
    red the center of the angle and the line \(\dot\sigma = 0\)
    blue a fitted or model quantity (force law, \(\pm 2\omega_0\), \(\pm\Delta a\))
    dark gray reference marks (turning points, jumps, \(h = 1\), \(a_*\))
    dark orange the warning line at the top of the legend when FAIR (m) is not drawn, with the reason
    cyan the catalog resonant axis \(a_{\rm res}\)
    orange, purple an accepted subresonance, the owner resonance

    The right column is ten blocks (header, subtitle, key facts, why, confidence, checks, evidence, caveats, numbers, footer; reading the figures), in two halves with a fixed place for each: header to checks on the left, evidence to the footer on the right, on every figure. The time axis is labeled under the last panel and under (b). Its plaque and ◆ take the status color (resonant #1B7A3A, transient #B35400, near_separatrix #6B3FA0, non_resonant #4A4F57, uncertain #2F5D9E, invalid #B2182B; style.STATUS_COLOURS, also used by the clone figures). The icons are ✓ green, ⚠ amber, ✗ red, ? gray and – light gray. The action is one chip in the header: gray no action needed, amber glance at (…), red review figure + numbers, blue extend to … or resample to …. A column that does not fit makes the figure taller, by at most style.COLUMN_MAX_GROWTH_IN (6 in); the left panels keep their place at the top. Beyond that the column trims (the Numbers groups first), and verdict_column.column_text gives the complete text of the column. Every time color bar uses the same map (viridis without its palest yellow). Wrapped angles are labeled in [0°, 360°). Time is in yr, kyr or Myr, whichever suits the span (the evolution figure keeps years).

    plot_options={'combined': {'numbers': 'all'}} prints every number of the decision in the right column of the combined figures instead of the ten blocks (explained, the default).

    The MMR figure has no empty slot: without FAIR the legend takes the place of (m) under one warning line that says why (mmr_combined.fair_warning). Panel (l) draws each accepted episode over its fill interval of (a), the part beyond its turning points dashed. Panel (e) draws the force law the status read (a witness episode's, rule R, at its own cutoff; under the direct averaged-force law its low-passed \(\dot a\) and \(s\times\) the term's force per window, mmr_combined.draw_direct_law). Accepted runs that are no libration of the angle are labeled #id wave, #id forced (strength) or #id ripple on (l) (mmr_combined.wave_labels; on (a) only a counted one is filled and labeled). The ▼ marks of the strip (b′) on (b) keep their x and step clear of the panel letter and the ALIAS plaque (mmr_combined.strip_marks). The MMR header's second line names who makes the cycles by its role (owner, driver, neighbor with its interval, resonant argument, kinematic term; verdict_text.cause_line); the episodes another resonance induces are hatched \\\\ on (a) (mmr_combined.INDUCED_HATCH, apart from the //// of an episode before a jump) and labeled induced on (l); also in … names the other resonance of a joint capture. Panel (a) of both the MMR and the secular/ZLK figure draws only what the minimal policy counts: the counted episodes (a covered edge filled to the record edge, the filter-trim stretch pale; a counted 1:1 leg labeled leg) and, faint with a dashed outline, the candidates cand; no rejected run and no gray strip (mmr_combined.policy_marks, shade_policy_runs; the ids come from the analysis' PolicyResult, or from the stored verdict's counted_episode_ids/candidate_episode_ids when a standalone panel re-runs the analysis without the scans); (l) is titled (l) episodes, counted solid and the numbers count N accepted, M counted. A 1:1 circulation judged on one leg or a non-1:1 half-swing between passages (leaves G4c, G4h), the flag extend_time, the 1:1 profile floor and a turn read in the edge trim are said in the right column (reading the figures). The 1:1 coverage audit's longest uncovered stretch (aud_unc_t0_yr–aud_unc_t1_yr) is a hatched gray strip xx at the foot of (a), labeled bounded, not counted (audit) (mmr_combined.shade_audit; flag uncovered_bounded_run). One 1:1 episode in parts: (a) brackets its sub-regimes over the panel, named by their well (mmr_combined.draw_subregime_brackets, run_label_items; overlapping names merge, merge_label_items), and hatches its inferred residence beyond the fill lighter, --, labeled inferred (residence_pieces, shade_residences; never on (l)); (d) marks the kicks of \(a\) at such changes with ▼ kick (draw_kicks); the header reads orbit: A → B for a sequence of wells; the EPISODES group of the right column lists the inferred stay and the marks (well, amplitude, kick, no well for the cycles beyond its well-holding core, rule coorbital_well_core) under the episode. Where a slower libration of the center was found (the slow scale, columns slow_*), (i) hatches its intervals (slow #id, mmr_combined.slow_intervals) and the right column's SLOW SCALE group gives its numbers; when its cause is a secular cycle, (j) adds the detrended \(p\varpi\) labeled with \(p\) and the secular period of its column (mmr_combined.varpi_overlay). A kinematic libration's (j) is \(\sigma = \lambda\) part \(+ p\varpi\) on the scale of that swing (mmr_combined.kinematic_scale).

    plotting.mmr_combined.draw_combined(analysis, series) draws the MMR figure from an analysis. After a restore without recomputation the analysis is run again on the saved series and checked against summary.csv; a difference is logged and stamped in red in the header.

    A figure for a paper: plot_options={'combined': {'figure': 'panels'}} (or draw_combined(analysis, series, options=...), draw_long_period_combined(..., options=...)) draws the panels only: no header, legend or right column, and no empty slot (an (l) without an accepted episode and an undrawn FAIR are left out, with no warning line; the portraits that remain move left and are lettered on from (k); the secular/ZLK figure does the same with its four squares, from (i)). The margin legends right of the panels stay, so the caption explains the rest. The page is cropped to what is drawn (12.45 × 12.8 in); a red stamp is still printed. The portraits keep their square size, so a row with one portrait leaves the rest of the bottom row blank. From a run or a restored one, fig = sim.data_manager.mmr_combined_figure(body, resonance, sim, options={'combined': {'figure': 'panels'}}) returns the MMR figure without saving it (fig.stamp: the mismatch with summary.csv, or None).

    Options¶

    plot_options (or PLOT_OPTIONS in .env as JSON) is merged over the defaults. Unknown keys and invalid values raise an error.

    option default meaning
    style screen standalone diagnostics only: screen (11 pt sans-serif, 150 dpi, a title) or paper (8 pt STIX serif, 300 dpi, no title; panels 88 mm, rows up to 180 mm). The width depends on the kind. The combined verdict figures keep their 24 × 13.5 in layout (taller when the right column needs it) and header in either style (MMR at 120 dpi; secular/ZLK saved at the style's dpi)
    raw line raw series next to filtered ones: line, markers, none
    series auto no effect (accepted for old configurations; no figure draws the standalone time series any more)
    recurrence.max_points None display decimation of the recurrence matrix; None = about 8 samples per cycle, within 700–2500
    recurrence.exclude_samples 2 blank band around the diagonal, in decimated steps
    portrait.y rate the MMR portrait's y axis: rate (\(\dot\sigma\)) or axis (\(a - a_0\))
    cycles.prominence None peak prominence in rad; None = 5 % of the range of \(\sigma\) within [0.005, 0.1]
    fair.max_step_fraction 0.25 the standalone fair figure warns (and still draws) when the output step exceeds this share of the orbit; panel (m) of the combined figure is not drawn above 1/4.2 of it
    combined.osculating_a full panel (d): full holds the osculating a in view, clipped fits the view to the filtered a, hidden also hides the osculating a beyond that view
    combined.numbers explained the right column: explained (the verdict in ten blocks: header with the status, level and action chip, subtitle, key facts, why, confidence pills, checks with their margins nested, evidence, caveats, numbers with the DECISIVE lines first, the footer of codes with the full tree's verdict) or all (every number of the decision)
    combined.figure full the page of the combined verdict figures (MMR, secular, ZLK): full (header, panels, legend, right column) or panels (for a paper: the panels and their margin legends only, see below)

    Redrawing after a run¶

    Nothing needs to be integrated again. Restore the run, then draw in any style:

    from resonances import SimulationSerializer, MMRPlotter  # noqa: E402
from resonances.plotting import SecularPlotter  # noqa: E402

sim = SimulationSerializer.restore('output/my_run/simulation.json')
body = sim.bodies[0]
plotter = MMRPlotter.from_body(body, body.mmrs[0], sim, options={'style': 'paper'})
plotter.plot('recurrence')  # recurrence, fair, portrait or cycles; the combined figure: plot_body below
plotter.save('recurrence.pdf').close()

secular = SecularPlotter.from_body(body, body.secular_resonances[0], sim)  # secular or ZLK rows: pass `sim`
secular.plot('portrait')
secular.save('portrait.png').close()

sim.config.plot, sim.config.plots = 'all', ['combined']
sim.data_manager.plot_body(body, sim)  # the verdict figures, from a re-run analysis

    MMRPlotter.plot draws the standalone kinds only (recurrence, fair, portrait, cycles); the combined MMR figure is redrawn by sim.data_manager.plot_body(body, sim) or draw_combined(analysis, series). MMRPlotter.from_body(..., focus=(t0, t1)) restricts cycles, portrait and the recurrence scales to an interval in years. Only bodies that passed the save filter have series on disk. MMRDiagnostics(...) builds the same figures from plain arrays (times in years; axis_analysis, the a of the cycles, of the portrait with portrait.y='axis' and of its reference \(a_0\), defaults to the osculating axis). A backward integration is put in time order internally; the axes show the actual negative times.

    Secular figures¶

    SecularPlotter (resonances.plotting.secular_plots; kinds angle, rate, portrait, combined) draws the angle the classifier read (the reduced proper angle by default, see Secular resonances) next to the angle of the osculating elements. In the combined figure, panel (d) is the semi-major axis (osculating band, filtered at \(P_c\), min – max; for a body in a planet's co-orbital zone, by \(a\) alone, the planet's \(a_P\) dashed and the zone's edges \(\pm k\,r_H\) dotted, ZLK_COORBITAL_HILL_FACTOR \(k\); secular_plots.coorbital_planet_geometric, for drawing only). Panel (g) of a secular angle draws the analysis' own response fit (the element and \(\sigma_f\) at the decisive cutoff, the center's when the center scale decided), the one whose \(R^2\) it prints (how to read it). For a Lidov-Kozai angle panel (g) draws the drift of the free and the osculating \(\omega\) in turns with a legend, as the free_omega drift figure. The right column has the ten blocks of the MMR figure; a ZLK row's Evidence asks what holds \(\omega\) (the forced vector, the center's slow wave, the node lock, the e–i coupling; Free elements: beyond the gate), a secular row's names the period seen on the angle against the FREQUENCIES rows, or unidentified. The numbers of both types include \(a\) in ORBIT and a FREQUENCIES table: the body's rates, the planetary modes (g5, g6, s6 always) and the beats, each in ″/yr with its period (how to read it). An induced ZLK libration (attributed to a forced mode, or a center at a beat of the forced vector) gets one more time panel, (a′) under (a): \(\omega_f\) with the forced direction and the dominant mode against the node (how to read it); every other row keeps eight. A ZLK figure reads \(\omega\), \(i\), \(\Omega\) and the free elements in the plane ZLK_ANGLE_FRAME names (the invariable plane by default; the header says which, panel (f) is i (invariable)). Below the time panels four squares: (i) the osculating portrait, (j) \(\sigma_f\) against its rate (ZLK: the free eccentricity vector), (k) the Charlier portrait of \(\sigma\) and \(e\) filtered at the decisive cutoff (secular_plots.filtered_charlier, charlier_radius), (l) the same per episode of (a); the legend is in a band under them, so this page is 1.45 in taller than the MMR one (how to read it).

    Eccentricity vector¶

    ecc_vector draws one figure per body (how to read it):

    resonances.EccentricityVectorPlotter.from_body(body, resonance, sim).plot().save('kh.png')
resonances.EccentricityVectorPlotter.from_data(times, ecc, omega, body_name='463').plot().save('kh.png')

    plot(limit=...) fixes the half-width, which is how the free-vector figure of Free elements is drawn on the scale of the osculating one.
